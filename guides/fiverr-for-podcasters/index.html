Why clips matter more than new episodes

Discovery on TikTok/Shorts feeds grows podcasts faster than episode count. At $5–$10 per clip, a month of weekly clips costs $20–$40 — the cheapest growth lever in podcasting. Pair it with our verified short-form editors guide when clips get serious.

Cover art: the $5 mistake to avoid

Podcast cover art runs $5–$65, but the cheap tier ignores platform specs — Apple Podcasts needs 3000×3000, and text must stay readable at thumbnail size. Confirm specs and readable-at-small test in the requirements before ordering; our illustration research shows the professional band sits at $25–$65.

Frequently asked questions
How much does podcast editing cost on Fiverr?

$10–$20 per audio episode and $35–$50 for video, verified from live listings. Top Rated editors with 1,000+ shows charge $20/episode; budget sellers start at $5 with longer turnaround.

Should I hire one editor for everything?

One editor for audio consistency, yes — episodes sound alike when one person mixes them. Cover art and clips can rotate: they are one-off jobs where a mismatch costs nothing.

What does a fully produced podcast month cost on Fiverr?

Four edited episodes ($40–$80) plus four clips ($20–$40) and occasional art ($15–$65 across the year): roughly $100–$200/month for a professionally produced weekly show.
