The fees, precisely

Buyers pay 5.5% of the order plus $3.50 when the total is under $200. That makes small orders expensive percentage-wise (a $5 gig costs $8.78 all-in — 75% in fees) and large ones cheap. The counterintuitive trick: $200 is cheaper than $199, because the $3.50 small-order fee disappears. Run your exact numbers in the fee calculator.

How escrow actually protects you

You pay Fiverr, not the seller. The money is held until you mark the order complete — and "complete" means you approved it, not that the seller delivered a file. If the work misses the agreed scope, the resolution path (revision → cancellation → refund) exists and works, which is the entire argument for platforms over DM hires. Just never approve something half-done to "be nice" — approval releases the money.

What seller levels really tell you

Levels are a volume + consistency signal, not a quality guarantee: Level 2 means 50+ orders maintained over months, Top Rated means Fiverr hand-picks, Pro means vetted professional background. But a Level 1 seller with 40 reviews all in your exact niche often beats a Top Rated generalist. Use levels to cut the pool, then let niche reviews decide.

First time buying on Fiverr?

Buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on orders under $200 — and a $200 order costs less than a $199 one (exact math here). Your money sits in escrow until you approve the delivery, and for anything over $500 you should split the work into milestones. The single best habit: order the seller's smallest package as a paid test before committing your real budget.

Frequently asked questions

What fees do I pay as a Fiverr buyer?

5.5% of the order value plus a $3.50 fee on orders under $200. A $100 gig costs $109; a $200 order costs $211 — less than a $199 order at $213.45.

Is my money safe on Fiverr?

Yes — payment is held in escrow and only released to the seller when you approve the delivery. If the seller misses the agreed scope, you can request revisions or a cancellation through Fiverr's resolution center.

Should I hire a Top Rated seller for my first order?

Not necessarily. Seller level signals order volume and consistency, not fit. A Level 1 or Level 2 seller with reviews specifically in your niche often delivers better than a Top Rated generalist.

What is the biggest first-order mistake?

Paying the full budget before testing. Order the seller's smallest package as a paid test — a $10 test reliably predicts how a $500 order will go.
