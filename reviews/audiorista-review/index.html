What you actually get

The no-code builder takes audio, video and PDF content — uploaded or ingested by RSS — and produces a branded app with your design, your browsing experience, push notifications and in-app purchases. Publishing goes through the App Store and Play Store under your brand. Pricing is tiered honestly: Demo at $29/month lets you build and preview a real app before committing; Lite at $240/month adds branded native iOS and Android apps with store publishing and in-app payments; Business from $400/month adds the branded web app, onboarding and advanced layouts. A 30-day free trial sits in front of all of it. The math to hold in your head: at $10/month per paying subscriber, Lite covers itself at 24 subscribers — everything above that is revenue you own instead of platform rent.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Creators, publishers, coaches and educators with an existing audience that listens on mobile — podcasts, audiobooks, sermons, courses — and wants direct subscription revenue under their own brand.

✘ Skip it if…

Web-only creators (Memberstack or Podia handle that for far less), audiences too small to clear the 24-subscriber math, and anyone unprepared for app-store review realities under their own brand.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • True white-label native apps — including CarPlay and Watch support — not a webview costume
  • No-code builder with RSS ingestion for existing podcast feeds

Frequently asked questions

What is Audiorista?

A no-code app builder for content creators: turn audio, video and text content into your own branded mobile and web apps — with subscriptions, in-app payments and push notifications — instead of publishing inside someone else's app.

How much does Audiorista cost?

Demo is $29/month (build and preview, no store publishing), Lite is $240/month (branded iOS and Android apps with in-app payments), Business starts at $400/month adding the branded web app and onboarding. A 30-day free trial is available.

Audiorista vs just using Spotify or YouTube?

Platforms give you reach but own the audience and the revenue split. Audiorista is the ownership play: your app, your subscribers, your pricing — it only wins if your audience will actually follow you into an app.

When does Audiorista pay for itself?

Simple arithmetic: at $10/month per paying subscriber, the $240 Lite tier breaks even at 24 subscribers. Below that, cheaper web-only membership tools are the honest answer.
