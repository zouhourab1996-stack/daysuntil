What you actually get

The pitch: citrus extracts (the marketing leans hard on Sicilian citrus and 'the citrus loophole') support metabolism and fat-burning that allegedly slows after 40. What you actually get is a bottle of capsules, a bundle-priced funnel (1/3/6 bottles), and a 60-day money-back window through ClickBank. The formula story is coherent and the product ships reliably — but the 'overnight' language on the sales page is classic VSL theater, and no clinical trial exists for this specific blend.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Women 40+ who already take supplements, want a simple daily capsule, and treat a new offer as a 60-day experiment rather than a promise.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone who needs clinical evidence before buying, dislikes long video sales letters, or is on medication without a doctor's sign-off.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • The freshest big-budget offer in the weight niche — current formulation, current funnel

Frequently asked questions

Is CitrusBurn legit or a scam?

It's a real, shipped product with a working 60-day refund policy through ClickBank. It is not a miracle: it's a citrus-extract supplement with no clinical trials behind the specific blend, sold with heavy marketing.

How much does CitrusBurn cost?

Around $69 per bottle at list, with bundle discounts for 3 and 6 bottles. Check the official site for today's pricing — these offers rotate frequently.

Is CitrusBurn FDA approved?

No — and no dietary supplement is. The FDA does not approve supplements; it regulates manufacturing. 'FDA-registered facility' on a sales page is a facility claim, not a product claim.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — 60 days from purchase, via ClickBank, regardless of the vendor's own policy.
