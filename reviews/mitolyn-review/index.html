What you actually get

You get a capsule built around maqui berry and a supporting cast marketed as 'mitochondria support'. The mechanism story is at least biologically literate (mitochondria do decline with age), and the official site is cleaner than most. The problem is the paper trail: public reviews skew heavily negative — complaints about results, refund friction and aggressive continuity — and our score reflects the gap between the pitch and the lived experience customers report. The vendor advertises an up-to-90-day guarantee; ClickBank's own 60-day window is the one you can rely on.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

People set on the mitochondria angle who accept the risk profile — and who will enforce the refund window without hesitation.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone expecting reliable results, or who reads public reviews before buying — because the public reviews are the reason this scores 6.4.
