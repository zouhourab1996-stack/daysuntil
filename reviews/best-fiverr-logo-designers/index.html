What a logo really costs in 2026

The "from $25" price is the teaser; what a business actually needs is the Standard tier: vector files, source files, and 2-3 concepts. Real budgets: $25-$60 for a clean minimal mark from a high-review seller, $60-$130 for business-ready with full file sets, $130-$400+ for Pro brand identity work. Add Fiverr's 5.5% + $3.50 - calculate the exact total.

How to choose

Match the portfolio to your industry - a luxury-minimal specialist will fight your brief if you need a playful mascot. Buy Standard, not Basic: the Basic tier of nearly every gig above delivers a PNG without source files. Ask about rights in writing: commercial use is standard, full copyright transfer often sits in higher tiers.

Red flags

  • Portfolio of clip-art assemblies - reverse-image-search a few samples before ordering.
  • "Unlimited concepts for $10" - volume pricing means template recycling.
  • No source files at any tier - you'll pay another designer to recreate it later.

When NOT to hire

If you need a logo this hour, Fiverr's own Logo Maker produces credible results from $30 with no waiting (see our Logo Maker vs Looka comparison). If the logo will sit on packaging or signage for a decade, the $385+ Pro tier is the honest answer, not the $25 one.

FAQ

How much does a good Fiverr logo cost?

A clean, professional logo from a well-reviewed designer runs $25-$60 at Basic tier, but the Standard tier most businesses need - vector and source files included - runs $60-$130. Pro brand identity work starts around $385.

Do I own the logo?

Commercial rights are included, but full copyright transfer is often only in higher tiers. Ask the seller in writing what rights you get before ordering, and check whether stock elements were used.

Which package should I buy?

Almost always Standard. Basic tiers usually deliver a PNG only - no vector or source file, which you need for print and scaling. Premium is for full brand kits with social media assets.

What's the difference between a $25 and a $385 logo?

The $25 tier gets you a competent minimal mark from a volume designer. The $385+ tier gets you a strategy process, original concepting, and a designer whose work has appeared on real brands. For a local business, $25-$60 is often enough; for a funded startup, it isn't.

How long does it take?

Most gigs in our table deliver first concepts in 2-5 days. Revisions add 1-3 days per round.
