What you actually get

You paste one line of JavaScript and get eight geo tools: Geo Redirect (send visitors to the right country page), Geo Links, Geo Content (swap images, text and HTML by location), Geo Bars and Popups, Geo Block for unwanted traffic, plus the newer Geo Consent, Geo Page and Geo Translate modules. Targeting is IP-based — reliable at country level, fuzzier at city level. Pricing works on pageview quotas, from the Dev tier ($11/month billed yearly, or $14 monthly) up to Enterprise ($335 yearly, $419 monthly), and every tier includes the whole suite on unlimited domains. A 14-day free trial lets you test it on real traffic first.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Multi-regional e-commerce and SaaS sites, and agencies managing multi-country client properties — anyone who needs region-specific offers, pricing, languages or compliance messaging.

✘ Skip it if…

Sites whose traffic is basically one country, anyone needing street-level precision (IP geolocation stops at country reliability), and anyone wanting a free plan — there isn't one.

Frequently asked questions

What is Geo Targetly?

A geo-targeting suite for websites: redirect visitors by country, show location-specific bars, popups and content, block unwanted traffic and localize compliance messages — all from one script and a dashboard.

How much does Geo Targetly cost?

Plans run on pageview quotas — from $14/month for the entry Dev tier up to $419/month for Enterprise on monthly billing ($11 to $335/month billed yearly; Startup $29, Growth $59, Business $119, High Traffic $239 monthly), with every tier including the full suite on unlimited domains. A 14-day free trial is available.

How accurate is the geo targeting?

It uses IP geolocation, which is dependable at the country level and noticeably less precise at city level — plan country-level rules accordingly.

Geo Targetly vs OptinMonster?

OptinMonster is a popup/lead-capture builder with geo among its triggers. Geo Targetly is the specialist: redirects, blocking, consent and whole-page personalization by location. If geo is the strategy rather than a feature, the specialist wins.
