What you actually get

Leads arrive from email, website forms, business cards, social profiles or a mobile app and land straight in a pipeline as an action to take — with calls, emails and meetings logged automatically on the lead's card. Reminders, prospecting lists and a mobile app built for field work carry the day-to-day; higher tiers add unlimited pipelines, quotes and invoices, and WhatsApp/VoIP integrations. The pitch is the name: everything that makes a traditional CRM feel like admin work was deliberately left out. Every plan starts with a 15-day free trial with full access and no credit card.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Small sales teams and solopreneurs who live in pipelines, hate typing data into a CRM, and want the tool to fade into the work.

✘ Skip it if…

Companies that need a full CRM — marketing automation, service desk, territory forecasting. noCRM is deliberately not that, and says so.

Frequently asked questions

What is noCRM.io?

Lead-management software for small sales teams: leads from email, forms, business cards and social flow into pipelines as next-actions, with activity logged automatically. It's a CRM stripped to the salesperson's workflow.

How much does noCRM.io cost?

The Starter plan runs about $12 per user per month billed annually, with higher tiers (Expert, Dream) adding unlimited pipelines, quotes and WhatsApp/VoIP. Tier pricing has shifted over the past year — check the official site before budgeting.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan comes with a 15-day free trial, full access, no credit card required. Adding payment details extends it to 30 days.

noCRM.io vs a traditional CRM like HubSpot?

HubSpot is a full platform — marketing, service, reporting at scale. noCRM covers only the salesperson's loop but removes nearly all the admin friction. Small teams that live in pipelines often close more with less; teams needing the full stack won't find it here.
