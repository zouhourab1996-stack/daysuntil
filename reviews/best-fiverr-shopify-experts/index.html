How to hire for ecommerce

1. Speed-test their past stores. Ask for two live stores they built and run them through PageSpeed Insights - ecommerce dies by load time. 2. Conversion over beauty. Ask what the seller does specifically for conversion (trust badges, cart drawer, mobile flow); a pretty template that doesn't sell is a cost. 3. Product copy is half the store - confirm who writes it; most cheap gigs expect you to.

Red flags

  • "7-figure store in 48 hours" - stores are businesses, not layouts.
  • Dropshipping clones of saturated products - you buy a copy of a store that already lost.
  • Apps installed without a budget plan - monthly app fees sink new stores faster than anything.

When NOT to hire

If you haven't validated the product, a $50 fast store to test is the right spend - not $1,300 before first sale. If you can write copy and drag blocks, Shopify's own themes plus a weekend gets you 70% of the mid tier. Hire experts for what templates can't do: speed, conversion structure and email flows that pay back.

FAQ

How much does a Fiverr Shopify store cost?

Budget test stores run $50-$90, professional builds $195-$250, and full agency setups $750-$1,300. Add SEO ($20-$50), product descriptions and email flows for a realistic total of $145-$870.

What should I ask a Shopify expert before ordering?

Two live stores they built (test them in PageSpeed Insights), what they specifically do for conversion rate, and who writes the product copy. Their answers separate builders from template installers.

Are Fiverr dropshipping stores worth it?

Test-tier stores ($50-$90) are worth it for validating a product fast. Pre-loaded 'proven' dropshipping stores usually sell you copies of already-saturated products - the product choice matters more than the store.

Do I need Klaviyo email flows?

For any store with real traffic, yes - abandoned-cart and welcome flows are consistently the highest-ROI marketing dollars in ecommerce. Mailvale's $250 full setup (825+ reviews) is the category benchmark on Fiverr.

Can a Fiverr expert migrate my existing store?

Yes - most Level 2+ experts in our table handle migrations (design, products, redirects). Ask specifically about 301 redirect mapping for SEO preservation; it's the detail that separates careful migrations from broken ones.
