What you actually get

Install the plugin and it audits your live Squarespace site page by page — meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, URLs, redirects, sitemap hygiene — then queues the fixes as clickable tasks you complete without leaving the editor. Around that core sit keyword research with tracked rankings, AI writing credits, backlink insights, and the standout: AI-visibility tracking, which monitors whether your pages surface in ChatGPT and other AI answers — a feature generalist SEO suites either lack or charge premium for. The ladder is honest: a genuinely free plan (50-page scans), Starter at $18.99/month for one site, Pro at $49.99 for three, Expert at $129.99 for ten sites with unlimited audits, and every paid tier starts with a 7-day trial without a card. The founder's own warning, posted publicly on Reddit, is the right frame for the whole category: a perfect audit score with no strategy behind it still earns zero traffic.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Squarespace site owners who want SEO guidance in the editor instead of a second dashboard — and designers/agencies managing multiple client sites (the 10-site Expert tier is effectively built for them).

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone not on Squarespace — the plugin simply has nowhere to live — and anyone expecting SEO on autopilot; the tool queues the work, you still have to do it.

Frequently asked questions

What is SEOSpace?

An SEO plugin built specifically for Squarespace: it audits every page of your live site from inside the editor and turns the problems into a prioritized, step-by-step fix list — plus keyword research, backlink insights and AI-visibility tracking for ChatGPT-era search.

How much does SEOSpace cost?

A free plan scans up to 50 pages. Paid plans: Starter $18.99/month (1 site), Pro $49.99/month (3 sites), Expert $129.99/month (10 sites, unlimited audits). Every paid plan starts with a 7-day free trial without a credit card.

Is SEOSpace better than Semrush or Ubersuggest?

For Squarespace sites, users consistently say yes — it audits in real time inside the editor and costs a fraction of the generalist suites. For cross-platform keyword research and competitor intelligence, the big suites still earn their price.

Will SEOSpace get me traffic by itself?

No — and the founder says so publicly. It finds and queues the fixes; ranking still requires acting on them and having a content strategy. Treat it as the work list, not the worker.
