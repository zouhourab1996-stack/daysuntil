What you actually get

A probiotic-and-mineral oral formula sold through a long-form 'the dental industry doesn't want you to know' VSL. Underneath the theater sits a reasonable idea — remineralization plus oral-microbiome support — in a category with early science. Fulfillment and the 60-day ClickBank guarantee are standard. The gap versus ProDentim is operational maturity and the sheer volume of overclaiming: 'remineralize teeth overnight' is not a thing any supplement does.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Buyers drawn to the remineralization angle who accept a newer operation behind it.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone allergic to conspiracy-flavored marketing, or expecting enamel regeneration.

Frequently asked questions

Can a supplement remineralize teeth overnight?

No. Remineralization is a slow, real process driven by minerals and saliva; nothing accelerates it to 'overnight' — that claim is the marketing, not the dentistry.

Is ProvaDent the same as ProDentim?

Different vendors, similar category — oral probiotics with mineral support. We score ProDentim higher on operation maturity and format.
