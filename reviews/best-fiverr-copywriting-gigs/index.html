How to hire a writer

1. Pay for a brief, not vibes. Give the word count, the audience, the one action readers should take, and two examples you like. 2. Buy one article first - judge research quality, not polish. 3. Check how they handle feedback - one revision round is where good writers prove they're professionals.

Red flags

  • "100% human-written" with AI-sounding samples. Read their portfolio aloud; AI prose has a rhythm.
  • Per-word pricing without a brief - length is the wrong unit; the job is.
  • No portfolio at all.

When NOT to hire

If the article is a commodity (product descriptions, local SEO pages), the $20 tier works. If the copy must sell - homepage, ads, launch emails - a $45+ conversion specialist pays for itself, and the cheap tier quietly costs you sales. And if you haven't defined the offer and audience, no writer can fix that for you.

FAQ

How much does a blog article cost on Fiverr?

Well-reviewed SEO articles run $20-$70 for 1,000-1,500 words; Pro-tier content with research and internal linking runs $70-$100. The $20 tier from a 1,000+ review seller is the best value on the platform.

How do I know if a writer uses AI?

Read their portfolio samples aloud - AI prose has a recognizable flat rhythm and vague transitions. You can also ask directly about their process; honest sellers will tell you where AI assists their research and where it doesn't.

What's the difference between content writing and copywriting?

Content writing informs (blog posts, guides) and is priced per article. Copywriting persuades (sales pages, ads, emails) and is priced per project at 2-4x. Buying content writing for a sales page is the most common - and most expensive - mistake.

Should I buy bulk article packages?

Only after a successful single-article test. Bulk deals lock in quality before you've seen it. Test one, then negotiate volume - most sellers will discount 15-30% for committed monthly work.
