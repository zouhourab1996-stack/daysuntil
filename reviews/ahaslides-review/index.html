What you actually get

Build a deck of interactive slides — multiple-choice quizzes, open polls, scales, spinner wheels, Q&A with upvoting — and your audience participates via a join code on their phones, with results appearing live on screen. It integrates into Zoom/Teams/Meet presentations without fuss, and the engagement mechanics (leaderboards, spinners) are the polished kind. As a full presentation designer it's not trying to compete with PowerPoint or Canva — decks here are containers for interaction, and it wins when the room's energy is the metric.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Teachers, trainers, workshop leaders and event hosts who need rooms to respond, not spectate.

✘ Skip it if…

People designing pitch decks — static design polish belongs to other tools; AhaSlides is for live interaction.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • The most generous free plan in the live-interaction category
  • Audience joins from a phone in seconds — no apps, no accounts
  • Quiz variety (live leaderboards, spinner, word clouds) keeps rooms awake
  • Works cleanly alongside Zoom/Teams/Meet

What could be better

  • Not a full presentation designer — import your visuals rather than building them here
  • Higher participant counts push you up tiers quickly
  • Export options are modest
  • Branding control needs the upper plans
