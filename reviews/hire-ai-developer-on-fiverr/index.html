The costs the gig price doesn't show

The build is a one-time cost. The AI keeps billing you every month. Fiverr's chatbot cost guide lists these recurring items:

  • Platform fees: chatbot platforms often start at $15 to $50 a month and grow to $100 to $500+ with volume.
  • AI usage: model APIs bill per request or per token, so cost rises with every conversation.
  • Maintenance: Fiverr estimates $100 to $500 a month for ongoing tuning on a busy bot, and $50 to $200 each time a connected tool changes its API.

Voice agents add per-minute fees. In September 2026 Myrado recorded published rates of $0.05 a minute for Vapi's platform fee (other providers billed on top), $0.07 to $0.31 for Retell and $0.12 to $0.14 for Bland. Its reading of 18 Fiverr gigs found that none of the voice packages said how many call minutes were included.

Ask one question before you order: "Whose accounts will the AI, the phone number and the workflows run on?" The right answer is always "yours".

How to vet an AI developer on Fiverr: 8-point checklist

1. Write 20 to 30 real test questions first

Take them from real customer emails or chats, with the answer you expect. Brief & Hire's review study found that projects go best when the job is defined by real questions and a pass mark agreed before work starts, not after the demo.

2. Write a "never do" list

What must the bot refuse or pass to a human every time? Refunds, medical or legal advice, prices it isn't sure of. Put this in the brief.

3. Ask for a live demo you can break

Screenshots and videos are easy to fake. Ask for a link to a working bot from a past project, then ask it awkward questions. Does it admit when it doesn't know, or does it make things up?

4. Check the stack matches the job

A good seller explains why they'd use a hosted builder (Botpress, Voiceflow) versus custom code (LangChain, OpenAI Assistants), and n8n versus Make. "I can build anything with any tool" is a warning sign.

5. Count different buyers, not reviews

On one shortlisted gig, Brief & Hire found a single buyer wrote 15 of 35 reviews. Repeat buyers are a good sign, but 35 reviews may mean 14 clients. Look for reviews from orders the size of yours.

6. Keep accounts and keys in your name

Your OpenAI or Anthropic API key, your n8n or Make workspace, your phone number. Share access, never ownership. Rotate any key you shared after delivery.

7. Pay by milestone on anything over two weeks

Fiverr supports milestone orders. Release each payment only when that milestone's tests pass. Bugs accepted in milestone one get built into milestone two.

8. Test before the order auto-completes

Fiverr completes an order automatically three days after delivery if you don't respond. Run your test questions in that window, not next week.

Red flags in AI gigs

  • "100% accurate" or "replaces your whole support team". No language model is error-free. Look for sellers who talk about fallbacks and handoff.
  • Asking for your owner logins or card details. Invite them as a collaborator or share a scoped API key.
  • Bot runs on the seller's account. If they host it, you're renting it. Leaving can mean a rebuild.
  • No mention of testing or support days. Myrado found only one of 18 gigs stated support days.
  • Pressure to pay outside Fiverr. You lose Fiverr's payment protection and the Resolution Center.

FAQ

How much does it cost to hire an AI developer on Fiverr?

Fiverr's 2026 marketplace data puts a typical AI chatbot project at about $216 and an AI agent project at about $295, with starting gigs around $100. Advanced chatbots with integrations run $800 to $2,000 or more. Fiverr adds a 5.5% fee, plus $3.50 on orders under $200.

What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions, usually from your FAQ or documents. An agent also takes actions, such as booking a slot, creating a ticket or updating your CRM. Actions need more testing and error handling, so agents cost more.

Are there monthly costs after the build?

Usually yes. AI model usage is billed per request, hosted chatbot platforms often charge $15 to $50 a month or more, and voice agents bill per minute. Fiverr estimates ongoing maintenance at $100 to $500 a month for busy bots.

How do I vet an AI developer on Fiverr?

Write 20 to 30 real test questions and a never-do list, ask for a live demo you can test, check reviews from orders your size, keep API keys and accounts in your name, and pay by milestone on larger projects.

Should the chatbot run on my accounts or the seller's?

On yours. Your AI provider key, your automation workspace and your phone number should be in your name, with the seller given access. If the seller hosts everything, leaving can mean a rebuild.
