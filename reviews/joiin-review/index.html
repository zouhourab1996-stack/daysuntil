What you actually get

Connect your Xero/QuickBooks entities and Joiin handles the work that consumes finance teams at every month-end: currency conversion, intercompany eliminations, and consolidated statements by entity, dimension or group. AI commentary drafts variance explanations you can edit. For a finance team of 1–20 people running multiple entities, it replaces the fragile master-spreadsheet that one person secretly understands. It's finance-specialist software — bookkeepers with a single entity need less than it offers.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Finance leads running 2+ legal entities across Xero/QuickBooks who currently consolidate in spreadsheets.

✘ Skip it if…

Single-entity businesses (your accounting software already is your consolidation) and enterprise groups needing full CPM platforms.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • Direct Xero and QuickBooks sync — no export/import rituals
  • Multi-currency and eliminations handled automatically
  • AI-drafted variance commentary is a real time-saver
  • Working free plan for trying with real books

What could be better

  • Consolidation logic (eliminations, FX) has a learning curve
  • Reporting depth below enterprise CPM tools
  • Smaller vendor — support is good not instant
  • Per-entity pricing needs mapping to your structure
