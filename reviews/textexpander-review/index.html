What you actually get

TextExpander does one thing extremely well: you type a short abbreviation, it expands into whatever text you define — email templates, code snippets, addresses, support macros with fill-in fields and optional scripts. It works across Mac, Windows, Chrome and iPhone/iPad, and the current headline is a free-forever plan, which makes trying it frictionless. For teams, shared snippet groups keep everyone's answers consistent (support, sales, healthcare, legal all use it heavily), and newer AI features surface the right snippet based on what you're working on in Chrome. This is a mature, focused product — category king for a reason.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Anyone who retypes the same content — support agents, recruiters, sales, developers, medical and legal staff — and teams that want consistent answers across every reply.

✘ Skip it if…

People who type unique prose all day (there's nothing to expand), Android-only users (no native app), and teams needing heavy workflow automation — this expands text, it isn't Zapier.

Frequently asked questions

Is TextExpander free?

Yes — TextExpander now has a free-forever plan covering core expansion features. Paid individual and team tiers add sharing, advanced fill-ins and admin controls; check the official site for current pricing.

Does TextExpander work on Windows and Mac?

Yes — native apps for Mac and Windows, a Chrome extension that covers ChromeOS and Linux workflows, and iPhone/iPad apps. There is no native Android app.

What is TextExpander actually good for?

Anything you type repeatedly: email replies, support answers, code blocks, addresses, signatures. Fill-in fields turn a snippet into a mini-form so each expansion is tailored in seconds.

Is TextExpander worth it for teams?

For support, sales, recruiting and medical/legal teams, shared snippet groups standardize answers and cut onboarding time. Pricing is per user, so measure that against hours saved.
