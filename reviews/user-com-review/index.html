What you actually get

Every visitor and contact gets a single timeline: page events, chat transcripts, email opens and custom attributes in one record, which the automation builder uses to trigger journeys across channels. Email, SMS, WhatsApp and web push share the same segmentation; live chat and chatbots sit on the same data; pop-ups, forms and landing pages are built in. Pricing runs from a €15/month email essential up through the suites — Campaigns at €69, Marketing Automation at €199, Customer Engagement at €499 and Performance at €999 per month billed annually — with add-ons for mobile marketing, loyalty, sales and service. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, no credit card.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Growth and marketing teams at SMEs consolidating point tools into one platform — with someone on the team who will own the automation work.

✘ Skip it if…

Solo founders who just need an email tool (cheaper dedicated options exist), and teams without anyone to build and maintain the workflows.

Frequently asked questions

What is User.com?

An all-in-one marketing automation platform: a unified customer-data timeline driving email, SMS, WhatsApp, web push, live chat, chatbots, pop-ups and automation workflows.

How much does User.com cost?

Per the site's pricing page: from €15/month for the email essential, with suites at €69, €199, €499 and €999 per month billed annually, plus add-ons. Verify on the official site — third-party listings lag behind.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — a 7-day free trial with no credit card required, enough to build your first automation and see it fire.

User.com vs HubSpot?

HubSpot is the safer default with a bigger ecosystem; User.com undercuts it on price at the suite level and puts chat, automation and one customer record in a single package. The trade-off is ecosystem size and support depth.
