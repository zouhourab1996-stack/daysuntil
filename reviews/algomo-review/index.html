What you actually get

The loop works in steps: visitors arrive with UTM and campaign context, get de-anonymized against account lists, and then see a website personalized to who they are — content, images, CTAs — while an AI inbound agent chats with them already knowing the answers' context. The same data extends to LinkedIn ads and outbound prospecting, which is what separates it from plain chatbots. Setup is a JavaScript snippet plus CSV/API audience imports. Pricing starts at $200/month with no per-seat charges and goes up through demo-gated custom tiers. One honest flag: the product lives on both algomo.com and newmode.ai, and even industry write-ups note the brand ambiguity — it's the same platform, but the dual identity is a confusion tax we wish they'd fix.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

B2B SaaS and services marketing teams with real traffic, a real budget ($200/month floor) and an ABM motion that currently duct-tapes chat, personalization and retargeting together.

✘ Skip it if…

Small sites and budgets (the entry price is $200/month), teams wanting a simple $20 chatbot — Algomo left that market behind — and anyone who needs published, self-serve per-MAU pricing before buying.

Frequently asked questions

What is Algomo?

A website conversion platform: it de-anonymizes visitors, personalizes the whole site per account, and runs an AI chat agent that knows who it's talking to — then extends the same data to LinkedIn ads and outbound. Note: it also operates on the newmode.ai domain; same product.

How much does Algomo cost?

Plans start at $200/month with no per-seat pricing, and scale through custom demo-gated tiers. There's no published free tier.

Algomo vs Intercom or Drift?

Intercom and Drift are chat-first platforms that added personalization. Algomo starts from visitor identification and personalizes everything — including the chat. Chat-first teams fit Intercom; conversion-loop teams fit Algomo.

Is there a free trial?

No published trial — evaluation runs through a demo. Treat the demo as the trial and bring real traffic numbers to it.
