What you actually get

The pitch is consolidation: find leads (lead explorer with filters), verify them, run AI-assisted email sequences, touch LinkedIn, and track it all in the built-in CRM with an AI copilot coordinating. When it clicks, it genuinely replaces three or four subscriptions for a small outbound team. The caveats are the young-platform classics: data quality varies by segment (B2B contact data decays fast everywhere), some modules feel earlier-stage than others, and pricing that starts at $169/mo assumes you'll use the whole stack — partial users should check whether point tools cost less.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Small outbound teams that want one subscription instead of four, and can absorb a young platform's edges.

✘ Skip it if…

Teams needing best-in-class in any single function (Woodpecker's deliverability, a real CRM) — bundles trade depth for breadth.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • Built-in lead validation catches decay before it bounces

What could be better

  • $169/mo entry price assumes whole-stack usage
  • Young platform: feature maturity varies across modules
  • Lead data quality varies by industry — trial it on your segment

Frequently asked questions

What is SalesTarget.ai?

An all-in-one AI outbound sales platform: B2B lead explorer, email sequences, LinkedIn outreach, CRM and lead validation.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $169/month (10,000 emails + 2,000 Lead Explorer credits) scaling to $639/month (500,000 emails + 7,000 Lead Explorer credits). A 30% discount applies to the first month for new customers on any monthly Email Outreach plan. Check the official pricing page for current details.

Does it replace Woodpecker + a lead database + CRM?

Functionally yes for small teams; individually each point tool is more polished. The bundle's value depends on how many functions you'd actually use.
