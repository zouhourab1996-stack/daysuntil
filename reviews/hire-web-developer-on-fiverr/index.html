Costs that sit outside the gig

  • Hosting and domain. WordPress needs a host; Webflow sells its own site plans. Memvers' Webflow guide lists $14 to $39 a month for most Webflow sites, and stresses that you, not the developer, should own the workspace.
  • Premium themes and plugins. Page builders, forms, SEO and backup plugins often have yearly licenses. Ask for a list before you order.
  • Maintenance. Updates, backups and small fixes. Fiverr's WordPress category data shows maintenance and bug-fix jobs averaging about $20 to $64 each, and Memvers puts monthly retainers at $200 to $1,000.
  • Content. Most developers don't write your copy or take photos. Our logo designer guide helps if you need branding first.
A $150 site that needs $40 a month in plugins and a rebuild in a year isn't a $150 site. Compare first-year cost, not the gig price.

How to hire a web developer on Fiverr: 8-step checklist

1. Write a one-page brief

List pages, features, example sites you like, your platform choice and your deadline. Fiverr's software development guide advises confirming deliverables, revision limits and post-delivery support in writing.

2. Shortlist three sellers who build what you need

A seller with 50 WordPress blogs isn't automatically good at a Next.js dashboard. Look for portfolio work that matches your platform and project size.

3. Open their live sites, not screenshots

Visit real URLs on your phone. Run one through Google PageSpeed Insights. Memvers suggests moving on if portfolio sites score below about 60 on mobile.

4. Ask one technical question

"Why would you use WordPress (or Webflow, or Next.js) for my project?" A good developer explains trade-offs. A weak one uses whatever they know for everything.

5. Start with a small paid task

For bigger builds, order one page or one component first. It tells you more about quality, speed and communication than any chat.

6. Keep ownership in your name

Your domain, hosting account, Webflow workspace, theme licenses and GitHub repository should all be yours. Give developers collaborator access, never your main password.

7. Use milestones on anything over a few weeks

Pay per milestone, and release each payment only after you test that part. Never pay 100% up front outside Fiverr.

8. Test before the order auto-completes

Fiverr completes an order automatically three days after delivery if you don't respond. Check every page on mobile, submit every form and confirm you can log in and edit before you accept.

Red flags in web development gigs

  • "I can build anything in one week." Realistic timelines include testing and revisions.
  • No version control on code projects. If the code isn't in a Git repository you can access, you have no safety net.
  • Nulled or "free premium" themes and plugins. They miss updates and can carry malware.
  • Hosting or domain registered in the seller's name. Leaving becomes hard and expensive.
  • Pressure to pay or talk outside Fiverr. You lose Fiverr's payment protection and the Resolution Center.

FAQ

How much does a web developer cost on Fiverr in 2026?

Well-reviewed WordPress gigs started at a median of $100 in September 2026. A 5 to 10 page business site usually costs about $300 to $1,000, and a custom React or Next.js web app starts around $1,000. Fiverr adds a 5.5% fee, plus $3.50 on orders under $200.

Should I choose WordPress, Webflow or React/Next.js?

Choose WordPress for blogs and content-heavy sites, Webflow for design-led marketing sites, and React or Next.js only when you need app features like user accounts, payments in the product or a custom dashboard.

How much does a Webflow site cost on Fiverr?

2026 guides put a Webflow landing page at about $100 to $400, a multi-page CMS site at $400 to $1,200 and complex custom builds at $1,200 to $3,000 or more. Webflow hosting is billed separately.

Is a $100 WordPress gig enough for a business website?

Usually not on its own. Starting packages often cover one page or a theme install. A business-ready site with several pages, forms, basic SEO and mobile testing usually lands in a higher package or a custom offer.

How do I protect myself when hiring a web developer on Fiverr?

Keep your domain, hosting and code repository in your name, give collaborator access instead of passwords, use milestones for larger builds, and test everything before the order auto-completes three days after delivery.
