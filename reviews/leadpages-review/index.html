What you actually get

The core loop: pick a template (hundreds, mobile-first), edit with a genuinely fast drag-and-drop, publish to your domain or theirs, and now run experiments without wiring up a separate testing tool. The AI page generation gets you a competent draft in under a minute, which you then fix. Unlimited traffic and leads on all plans matters more than people credit — overage anxiety is real with competitors. The honest costs: pricing has crept premium, the template aesthetic is recognizable, and website-building (vs page-building) is still an afterthought.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Marketers and small businesses that ship landing pages regularly and want testing in the same tool instead of a second subscription.

✘ Skip it if…

People needing one simple page forever (a $19 tool or even a free Carrd does that), and teams wanting a full website builder.

Pros & cons

What we liked

Frequently asked questions

How much does Leadpages cost?

Plans start around $99/month with annual-billing discounts; a 14-day trial precedes commitment. Check the site for current pricing.

Do I need A/B testing as a beginner?

Not on day one — but it's included, so you grow into it without buying another tool. Smart Traffic automates the optimization step.

Leadpages vs a free page builder?

Free tools build a page; Leadpages builds, tests and optimizes pages at volume. If you run paid traffic, the testing pays for the subscription.
