What you actually get

Sequences, personalization fields, follow-up automation — the table stakes — plus the differentiators: automatic warmup for new mailboxes, a built-in email verifier, deliverability monitors that alert you before a domain's reputation slides, and an agency panel for running client campaigns. LinkedIn steps complement email rather than pretending to replace it. The per-mailbox pricing model rewards focused campaigns over spray-and-pray, which is either a feature or a cost problem depending on your list discipline.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Outbound teams and agencies doing cold email who understand that deliverability is the whole game.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone hoping cold email is a volume game — Woodpecker's pricing and guardrails actively fight spam behavior, correctly.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • Deliverability tooling (warmup, monitoring, verification) included rather than upsold
  • Agency panel makes multi-client management genuinely workable
  • Condition-based follow-ups handle real reply logic, not just time delays
  • 14-day free trial on real campaigns

What could be better

  • Per-mailbox pricing multiplies fast if you run many sender accounts
  • Email is the focus — LinkedIn support is complementary, not equal
  • No built-in prospect database; you bring your own leads
  • Interface is functional more than delightful

Frequently asked questions

How much does Woodpecker cost?

Plans scale per mailbox from around $29/month, with a 14-day free trial. Check the site for current pricing.

Does Woodpecker include leads?

No — it sends campaigns to your lists (their built-in verifier helps clean them). Pair it with a lead source like SalesTarget for the full stack.

Why does deliverability tooling matter?

Cold email only works if it arrives. Warmup and monitoring protect the sender reputation your results depend on — most cheap tools skip this and your domain pays for it.
