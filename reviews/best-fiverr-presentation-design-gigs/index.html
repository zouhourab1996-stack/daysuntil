The $5-to-$425 spread, explained

Presentation design on Fiverr has the widest price spread of any category we cover: $5 animated-slide gigs to $250 template systems to $1,600 corporate-tier studios. What actually moves the price: number of slides, whether you need a reusable template (master slides) or a one-off deck, and whether copywriting is included. Most buyers overpay by ordering design when they need writing first — a designer makes ugly words pretty, not bad words good.

Investor decks: the one case where price buys realism

If the deck is raising money, the $25 tier is the wrong tool. Investor decks live or die on narrative structure (problem → wedge → traction → ask) and on data visualization that survives a partner's squint. The Top Rated sellers at the $100–$250 tier (Revyne Sol, Ha Phuong Tran) earn the spread by restructuring — and the honest ones will tell you your story is broken before touching the slides. That feedback alone is worth the premium.

Editable files — ask or regret

The most common complaint in this category: receiving a beautiful deck that is a stack of images — uneditable, unfixable, vendor-locked. Two words in your brief prevent it: editable source (PPTX with proper master slides, not flattened exports). Sellers at every tier will comply; almost none of them volunteers it.

FAQ

How much does a PowerPoint design cost on Fiverr?

Verified October 2026 pricing: animated slides from $5, full deck design from $25–$50, premium pitch decks around $100–$250, and template systems at the top of the band. Slide count, templates and copywriting are the price movers.

Can they write the content, or just design it?

Most gigs are design-only: they make your words presentable. Some pitch-deck specialists (the $100+ tier) restructure narrative as part of the service — check the gig scope or ask. If your content is unfinished, budget for a copywriter first.

Will I get an editable file?

Only if you ask. Put the words editable source in the brief: a PPTX with real master slides, not flattened image exports. It is the single most common complaint in this category and two words prevent it.

How fast can a deck be done?

Standard delivery is 2–5 days for most sellers here; several Top Rated sellers offer 24-hour delivery on small decks. Investor decks with restructuring need a week — rushing the thinking saves nothing.
