How to hire a voice that fits

1. Audition with YOUR text. Every serious VO seller does a short custom audition from your script. Send 3-4 lines of the actual script, not a description of it.

2. Define usage before price. 'Internal training video' and 'national YouTube pre-roll' are different products. Say where it runs and for how long.

3. Check the revisions policy. One pickup round is standard; unlimited free redos is not. Agree what counts as a revision (tone change = new order; mispronunciation = free fix).

Red flags

  • AI voices sold as human. Fiverr requires disclosure, but listen for flat prosody and check for raw-studio noise. Top sellers now advertise 'real human, not AI'.
  • No custom audition. A portfolio reel proves they can voice; only your text proves they can voice YOUR project.
  • Rights vagueness. If the gig doesn't say what license you get, ask in writing before ordering.

When NOT to hire a human VO

If the video is internal, disposable, or budget-zero, modern AI voices (including free ones) are legitimately good enough. If the voice needs to carry emotion - brand, story, comedy - a human still wins, and the $5-$20 entry tiers in our table make that test cheap.

FAQ

How much does a voice-over cost on Fiverr?

Entry tiers for short scripts run $5-$40, long-form YouTube narration $40-$150, and commercial work with usage rights $100-$500+. Most gigs price per 200 words. In our table, entry prices run $5 to $100.

What are usage rights in voice-over?

The permission for where and how long your recording can run. A voice for an internal training video costs far less than the same voice in paid YouTube ads or broadcast. Define usage before you agree a price - it changes the quote more than word count.

Should I hire a Fiverr Pro voice or a standard seller?

Standard sellers ($5-$40 entry tiers) cover most YouTube and explainer work well. Fiverr Pro (vetted) makes sense for brand commercials, high-volume narration and anything where a miss is expensive - the Pro artists in our table carry 2,000-8,000 reviews each.

Can I get a free audition first?

Yes - nearly every professional VO seller on Fiverr will record a few lines of your actual script before you order. Ask for it; a seller who refuses is telling you something.

Are Fiverr voice-overs AI or human?

Both exist, and AI must be disclosed. If it matters to you, ask directly and prefer sellers who advertise 'real human voice' - several in our table do. For emotional or brand-carrying reads, humans still measurably outperform AI.
