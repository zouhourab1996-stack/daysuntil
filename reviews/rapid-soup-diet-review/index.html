What you actually get

The 14 Day Rapid Soup Diet is a keto-aligned, soup-based rapid weight-loss plan sold on ClickBank (gravity ~12, $24.81 average sale — one of the more affordable front-ends in the diet niche). The mechanism is honest and old: low-calorie, high-satiety soups for two weeks produce a fast drop on the scale — a mix of fat, water and glycogen. The program gives you the soup recipes, the schedule and the shopping lists; the current sales page trades as "Flat Belly Soup Diet" (same vendor). As a reset or a kickstart it does what it says. As a lifestyle it is not designed — 14 days of mostly soup is a crash diet, and the research on crash diets is consistent: what comes off fast goes back on fast without a maintenance plan.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Someone who wants an inexpensive, tightly structured 14-day kickstart to break a plateau or kick off a longer plan.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone with a history of yo-yo dieting, and anyone seeking a sustainable long-term nutrition plan — this is a sprint, not a lifestyle.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 14 Day Rapid Soup Diet?

A keto-aligned, soup-based 14-day rapid weight-loss plan: low-calorie high-satiety soups, with recipes, schedules and shopping lists included.

How much weight can you lose on the soup diet?

Short very-low-calorie diets typically show a fast 3–7 lb scale drop in two weeks — a mix of water, glycogen and fat. Sustainable fat loss is slower; plan for the rebound risk.

Is the Rapid Soup Diet healthy?

For two weeks as a kickstart, most healthy adults tolerate it. As a long-term diet, no — it's too restrictive. Check with a doctor if you have any medical condition.

How much does it cost?

About $25 one-time — one of the cheapest structured diet programs on ClickBank.
