On top of that you pay Fiverr's buyer fee and your own Shopify plan. This guide shows the real 2026 numbers, what a good package must include, and the traps that turn a $60 gig into a $500 bill.

How to hire a Shopify expert on Fiverr: 7 steps

1. Write down your scope first

List your products (how many, how many variants), the pages you need, where you ship, and the payment methods you want. A seller can only quote what you describe.

2. Decide: DIY, AI builder, or freelancer

Fiverr runs a free AI Shopify store builder that creates a starter store tailored to a niche and syncs it to your Shopify account. Fiverr says it loads 10 products for your chosen niche and gives you a setup guide. Your Shopify subscription still applies. It's a fair option if you want a quick base and plan to polish it yourself, or hand it to a freelancer later. Try the Fiverr AI store builder (Sponsored · Fiverr affiliate link).

3. Shortlist 3 sellers

  • Open live stores from their portfolio on your phone. Most shopping happens on mobile.
  • Run one of those stores through Google PageSpeed Insights. Slow portfolio stores are a warning.
  • Read reviews from buyers with stores like yours, and check that recent reviews are strong.

4. Ask for a custom offer with everything written in

Ask for one total price that includes theme, pages, product uploads, payments, shipping and basic SEO. Memvers flags a common trap: a $50 to $100 "setup" gig with product uploads, SEO and theme edits all sold as extras. A clear $300 offer often beats a $60 base plus $400 in add-ons.

5. Give access the safe way

Never share your Shopify owner password. Every Shopify plan lets you invite third-party collaborators, so the seller can request access with only the permissions they need. Remove that access when the job is done.

6. Keep ownership in your name

The store, the domain, any paid theme license and every app subscription should sit in your Shopify account and be billed to you. If a seller buys a theme "for you", ask for the license in your name.

7. Test before you accept delivery

Fiverr completes an order automatically three days after delivery if you don't respond. Use that time: place a test order, check mobile layouts, check shipping rates for two locations, and confirm your policy pages are live.

Red flags in Shopify gigs

  • "Winning products guaranteed" or income promises. No one can promise sales. Treat profit screenshots as marketing.
  • Asking for your owner login or card details. Collaborator access is enough.
  • Pressure to move payment off Fiverr. You'd lose Fiverr's payment protection and Resolution Center.
  • Nulled or "free premium" themes. Unlicensed themes can break, miss updates or carry malicious code.
  • No portfolio stores you can visit. Screenshots are easy to fake. Live stores aren't.

FAQ

How much does it cost to set up a Shopify store on Fiverr?

Well-reviewed Shopify build gigs started at a median of $120 in September 2026, with most between $90 and $150. A complete setup with theme work, product uploads and basic SEO usually costs about $200 to $500, plus Fiverr's 5.5% fee and $3.50 on orders under $200.

Does the Fiverr price include my Shopify subscription?

No. You pay Shopify directly. In October 2026 the Basic plan costs $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly, and paid themes and apps are extra.

Is it safe to give a Fiverr seller access to my Shopify store?

Yes, if you use collaborator access instead of sharing your owner password. Every Shopify plan lets you invite third-party collaborators with limited permissions, and you can remove them after the job.

How long does a Shopify setup take on Fiverr?

The median delivery time for well-reviewed Shopify build gigs was 4 days in September 2026. Bigger catalogs, custom sections and revisions take longer.

Is Fiverr's AI Shopify store builder really free?

Fiverr says the builder is free to use and loads 10 products for your chosen niche. Running the live store still needs a paid Shopify plan.

Should I hire on Fiverr or an agency for Shopify?

Fiverr suits standard stores on a ready-made theme. For custom apps, ERP integrations or Shopify Plus checkout work, a senior Fiverr Pro developer or a specialist agency is the safer choice.
