How data entry is actually priced

Data entry is priced per record, per row or per hour — and the $10–$25 entry prices above almost always mean "per small batch", not "per project". The honest budgeting rule: for anything over 500 rows, ask for a per-1,000-row quote instead of the gig sticker. Every experienced seller on this list works from row counts and complexity (handwriting, multi-column matching, deduplication) rather than sticker prices.

The accuracy question — how to buy quality twice

The trick that separates a clean dataset from a cleanup project: order a paid sample first. Pick 100 rows, pay the small fee, and check the result against your own pass. Sellers with 400–4,500 completed orders (this list) are used to samples; sellers who refuse them are telling you something. Then structure the big order with a verification clause: a random 5% re-check before final delivery.

What data entry is not

Data entry sellers move data; they do not magically structure it. If your Excel file needs formulas, dashboards or cleaning logic (deduplication rules, fuzzy matching), that is a different skill — see our spreadsheet and automation guides. Ordering data entry for analysis work is the most common way buyers burn money in this category.

FAQ

How much does data entry cost on Fiverr?

Verified October 2026 pricing: entry gigs start at $10–$25 for small batches. For anything over 500 rows, ask for a per-1,000-row quote — complexity (handwriting, matching, deduplication) moves the price more than volume.

How do I make sure the data is accurate?

Order a paid 100-row sample first and check it yourself — experienced sellers expect this. Then include a verification clause in the main order: a random 5% re-check before final delivery.

Can they handle PDF to Excel conversions?

Yes — PDF-to-Excel is one of the most common jobs in the category (several sellers above list it explicitly). Scanned PDFs need OCR, which adds cost; digital PDFs are cheaper to convert.

What is the difference between data entry and data scraping?

Entry is manual typing or copy-paste from sources you provide; scraping is automated collection from websites (several sellers above do both). Scraping raises questions about a site's terms of service and data ownership — check that the source allows it before ordering.
