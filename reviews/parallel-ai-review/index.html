What you actually get

The frame is 'AI employees': agents trained on your business that carry ongoing jobs — prospect research, follow-up drafting, content pipelines, support triage — rather than one-shot chat answers. In practice it's an orchestration layer over LLMs with your context and tools attached, and for the right job (repetitive research, first-draft content, lead enrichment) it produces genuinely usable output. The honest picture: agents still need supervision, outputs drift without guardrails, and the product moves fast enough that features change under you. Entry pricing (~$15–25/mo) makes the experiment cheap.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Founders and lean teams with repetitive knowledge work to delegate and the judgment to review an agent's output before it ships.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone expecting hire-and-forget digital staff — current agents are juniors who never sleep but also never self-correct reliably.
