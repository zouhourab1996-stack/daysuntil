What you actually get

The platform handles the unglamorous machinery of an affiliate program — link tracking, attribution, commission payouts, affiliate onboarding — and adds the part most competitors lack: a marketplace of B2B SaaS affiliates who actually understand recurring-commission products. Setup is genuinely fast (a technical person can be live in an afternoon), the free tier covers a real program's earliest stage, and pricing scales with the revenue the program drives. It's not an enterprise partner-ecosystem suite, and it doesn't pretend to be.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

B2B SaaS founders and growth leads who want an affiliate channel without building the plumbing — especially with recurring commissions.

✘ Skip it if…

Consumer-product merchants (this marketplace is B2B SaaS-first) and enterprises needing full partner-ecosystem management.

Pros & cons
