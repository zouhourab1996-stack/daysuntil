What you actually get

This is a digital program: a 21-day schedule that replaces two meals a day with specified smoothies, built on ordinary grocery-store ingredients, plus recipes, shopping lists and a swapping guide. No autoship, no capsules, no 'proprietary blend'. The trade-off is the obvious one: it requires actual participation — shopping, blending, and not eating the things the plan replaces. Compliance is the product. As a program design it's sensible and flexible; as a business it's refreshingly simple, with a 60-day ClickBank guarantee on a purchase you can evaluate fully within a week.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

People who do better with a structured plan than a pill, own a blender, and want a defined 21-day reset.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone hoping for passive weight loss, or who hates drinking meals — the failure mode here is buying the plan and ignoring it.
