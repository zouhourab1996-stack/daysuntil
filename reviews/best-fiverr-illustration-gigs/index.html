How to commission art you'll love

1. Reference beats description. "Like image A's colors, image B's outfit" is 90% of a good commission. 2. Confirm commercial rights before ordering - personal-use-only is the default on cheap tiers; merch and book covers need the rights tier. 3. Ask about the queue - the best artists are booked; a two-week wait usually means quality.

Red flags

  • Portfolio images that don't match one style - stolen portfolios exist; reverse-image-search before paying.
  • "Any style you want" - specialists beat generalists at every price.
  • No sketch preview in the process. Reputable artists show a sketch before finishing.

When NOT to commission

If it's for a profile picture with no attachment, AI generators are free and fine. If you're building a brand (mascot, game, book series), commission a human - consistency across pieces is what you're actually paying for, and AI can't hold a character model across ten illustrations.

FAQ

How much does custom character art cost on Fiverr?

Avatars and profile pictures run $5-$25, full character illustrations $25-$65, professional concept design $65-$150, and VTuber models with rigging $50-$300+. In our table, entry prices run $5 to $65.

Do I own the art I commission?

Personal use is usually included; commercial rights (merch, book covers, game assets) are typically a separate tier. Confirm in writing before ordering - it's the #1 dispute in this category.

What should I send when commissioning?

Reference images beat descriptions. Send 2-3 images showing the style, colors and outfit you want, one sentence on the character's personality, and the intended use. That brief alone raises your odds of a great result dramatically.

How long do commissions take?

Most artists in our table deliver in 3-7 days; the best are booked 1-2 weeks out. A queue is usually a good sign.

AI art or a human artist?

AI is fine for throwaway avatars. For anything with a character that must stay consistent across multiple pieces - a brand mascot, a book series, a game - a human artist is the only option that holds the model.
