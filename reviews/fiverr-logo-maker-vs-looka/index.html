Where Looka wins

  • Price for editable files. Looka Premium costs $65 and includes unlimited post-purchase changes. Fiverr's matching tier, Unlimited, costs $90.
  • Cheaper entry point. At $20, Looka's Basic package is $10 below Fiverr's $30, though it gives you only one low-resolution PNG.
  • Lifetime support and file formats. Looka lists PNG, EPS, SVG and PDF files in Premium, plus transparent-background variations.
  • A real branding ecosystem. If you want business cards, letterheads and invoices that match, Brand Kit does that in one place.

Where Fiverr Logo Maker wins

  • Style guide and social kit at $60. Fiverr includes a brand style guide, social media kit, Zoom backgrounds and favicon in the Professional package. Looka puts templates behind a yearly subscription.

  • Designer-made templates. Fiverr says the library is built on original designs by its freelancers, rather than generated from scratch.

Rights and trademarks: read this before you buy

Both companies let you use the logo commercially. Neither one is a safe choice if you plan to trademark it.

  • Fiverr: the Logo Maker license is non-exclusive. Other businesses can end up with similar templates, so you cannot count on the design being unique.
  • Looka: its FAQ says you have full copyright of the logo as a whole, but not of individual elements such as symbols. It also tells buyers who need a legally binding trademark to contact their local trademark office.
If you require a legally binding trademark and want to prevent others from using your logo, please contact the copyright/trademark office in your country. Looka Pricing FAQ, looka.com/pricing

If a unique, ownable mark matters to you, a custom logo from a vetted designer is a better path than any logo maker. This is general information, not legal advice.

Who should pick which

  • Choose Looka Premium if you want vector files, unlimited edits and the lowest total price, and you can live without a ready-made style guide.
  • Choose Fiverr Logo Maker Professional if you want the style guide and social kit included, you are fairly sure of your design, and one extra revision is enough.

A simple 15-minute test

  1. Type your business name into both tools. Do not pay yet.
  2. Save your three favorite designs from each.
  3. Show all six to two people who do not know which tool made them.
  4. Check which files you would need: print, website, social, or all three.
  5. Pick the cheapest package that includes those files, and screenshot the checkout page for your records.

Five mistakes to avoid with any logo maker

  1. Buying the cheapest tier by reflex. A $20 or $30 download can be a single PNG. If you need print or a website header that scales, you will pay again to upgrade or start over.
  2. Skipping the color test. Check your favorite design in black and white, and on both a light and a dark background. A logo that only works in color is hard to use on invoices, stamps and embroidered shirts.
  3. Ignoring file types. Vector files (SVG, EPS, PDF) stay sharp at any size. PNG files do not. Ask which files you will use, then match the package to that list.
  4. Forgetting that similar logos exist. Templates and generators reuse shapes and layouts. Search an image of your logo before you print 1,000 business cards, and consider a custom design if you work in a crowded field.
  5. Assuming a subscription is optional forever. Looka says you keep ownership of your logo even after a Brand Kit subscription ends, but the subscription is what pays for ongoing Brand Kit access. Decide whether you really need the extras before the first renewal.

FAQ

Is Fiverr Logo Maker better than Looka?

It depends on the package. Fiverr's $60 Professional tier includes a style guide and social media kit, while Looka's $65 Premium gives unlimited edits and four file formats. For editing flexibility Looka is cheaper; for extras in the box Fiverr is.

How much does Looka cost in 2026?

Looka lists a Basic logo (one low-resolution PNG), a Premium logo (PNG, EPS, SVG, PDF and unlimited changes) and Brand Kit subscriptions. Third-party trackers put these at $20, $65 and about $96 a year, so confirm at checkout.

Can I edit my logo after I buy it?

On Fiverr Logo Maker, not on the $30 tier, one extra revision on the $60 tier and unlimited on the $90 tier. On Looka, Premium includes unlimited post-purchase changes, and you can change everything except the company name.

Will I own the logo?

You can use both commercially. Fiverr's license is non-exclusive. Looka gives you copyright of the logo as a whole but not of individual elements such as symbols. Neither is a reliable route to a trademark.

Is it free to try either tool?

Yes. Both let you design and preview for free and charge only when you download your files.

Should I hire a designer instead?

If you need a unique mark you can trademark, yes. A logo maker is best for a fast, affordable start.
