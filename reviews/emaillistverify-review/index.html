What you actually get

Upload a list or hit the API and every address gets checked against syntax, domain, disposable-provider, catch-all and known-trap signals, sorted into deliverable/risky/undeliverable buckets. It integrates into signup forms (real-time validation) and ESP workflows, and the accuracy holds up against the big names at a friendlier price curve. The value math is blunt: bounce rates above ~2% damage sender reputation, and one prevented bad send pays for a year of verification. The dashboard is utilitarian and the brand is invisible — neither matters at 8.1.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Anyone who emails a list — cold outreach or marketing — who hasn't verified it in the last quarter.

✘ Skip it if…

Nobody, really — this is maintenance-level tooling. If you email at all, this category applies.
