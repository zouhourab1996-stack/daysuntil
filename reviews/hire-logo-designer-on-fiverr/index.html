Why vector matters: a vector logo scales to a shop sign or a t-shirt without going blurry. A PNG-only logo is fine for a profile picture. It's a problem the day you order business cards.

Rule of thumb: if the logo will ever be printed, buy the tier that includes a vector file and the source file. Don't plan to "ask nicely" for them later.

How to hire a logo designer on Fiverr: 8 steps

1. Decide where the logo will live

Website and social only? A Basic tier can work. Packaging, signs, uniforms or print? You need vector and source files, so plan for Standard or higher.

2. Set your all-in budget

Pick a package price, then add the fee. A $90 package costs $98.45 at checkout. Knowing the real number keeps you from upgrading mid-order.

3. Filter the category the smart way

  • Seller level: Level 2 or Top Rated is a reasonable floor for business work. Fiverr Pro badges mean Fiverr vetted the seller.
  • Budget filter: set it to your Standard-tier budget, not the cheapest price.
  • Delivery time: 2 to 5 days is normal. Pay for rush only if you truly need it.

4. Read the portfolio like a buyer, not a fan

Look for range. If every sample uses the same gradient swoosh, you'll get the same swoosh. Open the reviews and look for buyers in your industry. Then check that recent reviews (last 2 to 3 months) are as good as the old ones.

5. Message the seller before you order

Send a short brief and ask three questions: Is this drawn from scratch? Which file types do I get in this package? Do I get full commercial rights or a copyright transfer? A clear, fast answer is a good sign.

6. Order the right package with extras included

Add extras like extra concepts or a social kit at checkout. Extras added later are separate payments, and each one carries its own service fee.

7. Review fast

Fiverr marks an order complete three days after delivery if you take no action. Block time to review the concepts as soon as they land, and use your revisions with clear, written notes.

8. Check the final files before you accept

  • Vector file opens and scales cleanly (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF)
  • Transparent PNG versions in color, black and white
  • Fonts named, or outlined in the file
  • Written confirmation of commercial use or copyright transfer

A copy-paste logo brief

  • Business name and tagline (if any)
  • What you sell and to whom, in one sentence
  • Three words for how the brand should feel (for example: calm, premium, local)
  • Colors you like and colors to avoid
  • Two or three logos you admire, and why
  • Where it will be used: web, print, packaging, signage
  • Files you need and your deadline

Red flags to avoid

  • "Unlimited concepts for $5." Real custom concepts take time. Very low prices with unlimited promises often mean templates or recycled work.
  • No mention of file types. If the package page doesn't list vector or source files, assume you won't get them.
  • Vague answers about originality. Ask directly whether stock icons or AI images are used. Stock elements can limit how you use or protect the logo.
  • Pressure to pay outside Fiverr. Never do it. You lose Fiverr's payment protection and the Resolution Center.
  • Reviews that only praise speed. "Fast delivery!" tells you nothing about design quality.

Important: A design package is not trademark protection. Clearance searches and trademark filing are separate legal steps with their own fees. If the name and logo matter for the long run, talk to a trademark professional in your country.

Pros and cons of hiring a logo designer on Fiverr

What works

  • Huge choice of styles and price points
  • Fixed package prices, so the budget is capped
  • Fast delivery, often 2 to 5 days
  • Payment held by Fiverr until you accept
  • Logo Maker gives a quick option from $30

Watch out for

  • Quality varies a lot between sellers
  • Cheap tiers often skip vector and source files
  • The fee adds about 18% to a $27.50 order
  • No general money-back guarantee outside Pro
  • Template or stock-based work isn't always disclosed

Who should skip Fiverr for a logo

If you need a brand strategy, naming, market research and a full identity system, a specialist studio may serve you better than a single gig. The same goes if you can't describe what you want yet. A designer can't fix a missing brief. Start with the brief above, and come back when it's clear.

FAQ

How much does a logo cost on Fiverr in 2026?

Well-reviewed logo gigs started at a median of $27.50 in September 2026, but a business-ready Standard package with vector and source files usually costs about $60 to $130. Fiverr then adds a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on orders under $200.

Is a $5 logo on Fiverr worth it?

Rarely for a real business. Very cheap packages usually deliver a PNG only, with few revisions and no vector or source file, which you need for print, signs and packaging. They can work for a quick test or a social profile image.

Do I own the logo I buy on Fiverr?

Fiverr gigs include commercial use rights, but full copyright transfer is often listed only in higher tiers. Ask the seller in writing what rights you get before you order, and check whether stock elements were used.

Should I use Fiverr Logo Maker or hire a designer?

Logo Maker is fast and costs $30, $60 or $90, but it is template-based and the $30 plan is PNG only. A designer costs more and takes a few days, but gives you a custom mark. For a long-term brand, hire a designer.

How long does a Fiverr logo take?

The median delivery time for well-reviewed logo gigs was 2.5 days in September 2026. Revisions add time, and Fiverr completes the order automatically three days after delivery if you take no action.

Is Fiverr Pro worth it for a logo?

It is worth it for high-stakes work like packaging or a funded launch. Pro logo gigs started at a $130 median in a September 2026 check, and eligible Pro orders carry a 14-day money-back guarantee paid as Pro balance.
