What you actually get

The platform solves the 'why don't users understand our app' problem three ways: interactive walkthroughs that guide users through procedures inside your application, a knowledge-base and document library for self-help, and an AI chat assistant that answers from those same materials instead of hallucinating. Integration is a script tag or a browser extension, and the free beta plan is genuinely usable. It's a small vendor: fewer templates, fewer integrations and a plainer finish than Appcues/UserGuiding — at a fraction of the cost, which is the entire proposition.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Product teams at small SaaS companies wanting onboarding walkthroughs and self-serve help without enterprise pricing.

✘ Skip it if…

Teams needing the ecosystem depth (segmentation, analytics, integrations) of the established digital-adoption platforms.
