What Leavo actually is

Most leave-management tools solve one slice: requests and approvals. Leavo's pitch is the whole loop — leave → time tracking → shift planning → overtime banking → paid time off, automatically. A published shift fills the timesheet; validated hours bank as overtime; overtime converts to leave days without a spreadsheet in between. Each edit keeps a before/after audit trail, and the platform detects seven types of timesheet discrepancies on its own.

The differentiator is the AI assistant: employees ask about balances, team status or policies in plain language — or by voice — and book leave with a single confirmation. Crucially, Leavo says it's permission-safe: the assistant respects every access role and "never guesses." That's the right design; verify it against your own permission matrix during the trial.

Don't confuse it with Levo.ai

A warning that will save you an hour: Leavo (leavo.com) is HR software. Levo.ai is an API-security platform. They are unrelated companies with near-identical names. If a review you find talks about API threat detection, it's the other one. This name collision is genuinely bad luck for both — and one reason Leavo's independent review footprint looks thinner than it may deserve.

FAQ

What is Leavo?

Leavo (leavo.com) is an all-in-one HR platform for leave management, time tracking, shift planning and overtime banking — one connected flow instead of separate spreadsheets and point tools. It includes an AI assistant that answers balance and policy questions and books leave by chat or voice.

How much does Leavo cost?

A 30-day free trial (no card) with unlimited members, then Professional at $448/year (about $2.49 per user/month for a 15-member team) and Premium at $747/year (about $4.15 per user/month) with unlimited organizations, AI credit pools and a dedicated manager. Vendor-published prices — confirm at checkout.

Is Leavo the same as Levo.ai?

No — and this confusion is real. Leavo (leavo.com) is HR/leave-management software. Levo.ai is a completely different product for API security. If you search 'Leavo reviews' and find API-security content, you're looking at the wrong company.

Is there a free plan?

No permanent free plan — a 30-day full-access trial with unlimited members and no credit card. After that, both paid tiers are per-user priced with annual billing.

Who should skip Leavo?

Teams that only need simple absence tracking (a shared calendar does that free), anyone who needs monthly billing or a permanent free tier, and organizations requiring a long public track record — Leavo is a newer product with few independent reviews so far. Run the trial against your real policies before committing.
