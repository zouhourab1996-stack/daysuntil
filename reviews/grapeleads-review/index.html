What you actually get

You drag a rectangle over a map area, type a business category, and the platform pulls live Google Maps results — not a database compiled last year — filtered by whether the business lacks a website or runs an outdated one. Each lead gets enrichment: contact details, web presence, SEO signals, reviews and operational status, with email verification built in so you're not bouncing into spam. Exports flow to CSV, CRMs and webhooks. Pricing is credit-based and pay-as-you-go — packs from $28 with no monthly lock-in, which is the right shape for a prospecting experiment.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Web designers, SEO and marketing agencies, and freelancers whose ideal client is the long tail of local businesses that still need to get online.

✘ Skip it if…

Enterprise B2B teams — Apollo and ZoomInfo own that ground — and anyone looking for a full outreach suite; this finds leads, it doesn't run your campaigns.

Pros & cons

What we liked

  • The no-website and outdated-website filters target prospects nobody else is pitching
  • Live Google Maps data instead of stale pre-built lists
  • Built-in email verification saves a separate tool subscription
  • Credit packs from $28 with no monthly commitment — cheap to test honestly

What could be better

  • Deliberately single-purpose — useless outside the sell-web-services motion
  • Businesses without websites are by definition harder to research and contact
  • Depends entirely on Google Maps data quality in your target area
  • Small vendor with a thin independent track record — we score what's provable

Frequently asked questions

What is Grape Leads?

A lead-finding tool for web agencies: it scans Google Maps for businesses without websites (or with outdated ones), enriches them with contact and web-presence data, and verifies emails — so agencies know exactly who needs their services.

How much does Grape Leads cost?

Pay-as-you-go credit packs starting at $28, with larger packs around $55 and $127 — no monthly subscription required. Check the official site for current pack sizes.

How fresh are the leads?

They're pulled live from Google Maps at search time rather than from a pre-built database — the difference between current data and a list compiled a year ago.

Grape Leads vs Apollo or ZoomInfo?

Different sports. Apollo and ZoomInfo sell enterprise-scale B2B databases. Grape Leads sells one hyper-specific thing: local businesses that visibly need a website. If you sell web services, the specialist is cheaper and sharper.
