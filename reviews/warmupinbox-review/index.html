What you actually get

Connect a sending inbox (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or any custom SMTP — setup is genuinely about thirty seconds), set a daily volume, and the platform's network of 30,000+ real inboxes starts exchanging realistic mail with it: opened, replied-to, marked important, and pulled back out of junk where it landed — the "spam rescue" move that quietly teaches filters you belong in the primary inbox. Tiers scale the volume: Basic covers 75 warmup mails a day, Pro ($49/mo annual) raises that to 250 with Gmail-, Outlook- and Yahoo-specific targeting plus custom templates, and Max ($79/mo annual) goes to 1,000 a day with API access. Over two to four weeks this ramps a cold mailbox into a sender spam filters tolerate, with reputation monitoring so you watch the climb instead of guessing. The honest caveat: warmup is increasingly bundled into sending tools (Woodpecker ships it built-in), so this is the tool for senders whose stack doesn't include it — or for warming more mailboxes than a sender's bundled allowance covers.

Who it's for — and who should skip it

✔ Made for you if…

Cold-email teams and agencies with new domains or mailboxes to prepare — especially anyone whose sending tool doesn't include warmup, or who warms many inboxes at once.

✘ Skip it if…

Anyone not sending cold email (regular newsletter platforms manage their own reputations), and users of tools that already bundle warmup — check before double-paying.

Frequently asked questions

What is WarmupInbox?

An email warmup platform: its network of 30,000+ real inboxes exchanges realistic mail with your sending mailbox — opens, replies, spam-rescue — to build the sender reputation cold email depends on.

Do I need it if my outreach tool has warmup built in?

Maybe not — Woodpecker and several modern senders include warmup natively. Check your tool first; WarmupInbox earns its subscription when your stack lacks it or you need more warmed inboxes than the bundled allowance.

How much does WarmupInbox cost?

Basic is $15 per inbox per month billed annually ($19 monthly) for 75 warmup emails a day; Pro is $49 annual ($59 monthly) for 250 a day plus provider-specific targeting and custom templates; Max is $79 annual ($99 monthly) for 1,000 a day plus API access. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial without a card — check the official site for current pricing.

Is WarmupInbox better than Warmbox?

It is cheaper ($15 vs $19 per inbox) and offers a no-card free trial, which Warmbox does not. Warmbox counters with a larger network (35,000+ inboxes) and customizable warmup recipes. For bare-bones warming on a budget, WarmupInbox wins; for deeper control and monitoring, Warmbox justifies the extra $4.

How long does warmup take?

Plan on 2–4 weeks before a new mailbox is ready for real campaign volume — reputation is earned gradually, and any tool promising overnight trust is selling you impatience.
