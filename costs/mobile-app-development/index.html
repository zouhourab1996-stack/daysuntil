The Fiverr tier, honestly

Fiverr's app development tier is real but narrow: our verified web & app development research found custom application builds from $500–$3,000+ with review histories that check out. In this band you're buying an MVP on a cross-platform stack (Flutter or React Native) built from a near-final design, not product strategy. It works when you already know exactly what the app does — screens listed, flows drawn — and you want it built, fast.

The three checks before paying any Fiverr app developer: one, open their published apps in the actual app stores (not screenshots); two, the gig must say "source code included"; three, milestone delivery — never 100% upfront. Do those three and the $2,000 route is genuinely low-risk for an MVP.

When you genuinely need the agency

You need agency pricing when the app is the business: payments at scale, HIPAA or finance compliance, real design research, QA across device matrices, and a team that's still there in year two. You also need it when you can't specify the product yourself — agencies sell product thinking as much as code. But if your app is a well-defined tool, directory, or companion to an existing business, a $2,000–$5,000 MVP that you can put in front of users this month beats a $60,000 perfect build next summer.

What it costs after launch

After launch, budget: Apple Developer $99/year, Google Play $25 once, push/notification services $0–$50/month, and maintenance — every OS update breaks something. Freelancers typically quote 15–20% of build cost per year for maintenance; factor it before comparing quotes. Year-one realistic total for a Fiverr MVP: $1,500–$7,000 all-in.

First time buying on Fiverr? Read this first

Most people researching "how much does X cost" have never ordered on Fiverr. Three things worth knowing: one — buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus a $3.50 small-order fee under $200 (calculate your exact total); two — payment sits in escrow until you approve the work; three — for any build over $500, split it into milestones. Start with the seller's cheapest package as a paid test before committing the full budget.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build an app in 2026?

A working MVP costs $500–$5,000 from a Fiverr developer, $6,000–$80,000 from a direct freelancer, and $30,000–$150,000+ from a US agency. Complex enterprise apps run $150,000–$500,000+. Most production-ready agency MVPs land between $50,000 and $150,000.

Can I really get a working app for $500 on Fiverr?

You can get a working MVP for $500–$2,000 if you bring finished designs and a complete feature list — the seller builds, you direct. What you cannot get at that price: product strategy, design, backend scale, QA across devices, or maintenance. Treat it as a prototype you own.

Why do agency quotes vary from $30k to $500k for similar apps?

Mostly labor rate and scope definition. US agencies bill $125–$200/hr effective; offshore agencies 40–70% less. Enterprise consultancies add compliance and integration work. Quotes under $30k from US agencies almost always mean the scope isn't defined yet — the number will grow.

Flutter/React Native or native iOS/Android?

Cross-platform (Flutter, React Native) is the right default for 90% of apps — one codebase, both stores, and the Fiverr tier is built on it. Go native only for heavy animation, AR, or when a platform API is brand new and cross-platform bindings lag.
