What to check before you buy in 🛠️ B2B SaaS Tools (Reditus)

We have not tested Signitic, so we will not tell you what it does. What we can do is tell you what goes wrong in this category, which is where the money usually goes:

  • Ask whether the price you see is per seat or per workspace, and what the minimum seat count is — the gap between the two is where most SaaS budgets break.
  • Check what happens to your data if you leave. Export in a usable format, on a plan you can actually afford, is the difference between a tool and a hostage.
  • Find out whether there is a self-serve trial or only a sales demo. Demo-only usually means pricing you cannot see until you have already spent an hour.
