Joint Genesis: Verified Facts Before You Buy

A once-a-day joint supplement built around Mobilee (a hyaluronic acid matrix), pine bark extract, ginger, boswellia and BioPerine. Here is what we could verify about the company, the refund and the safety cautions.

Listing, not a hands-on review: we have not bought or used Joint Genesis. Everything below is from the sources linked in each line.

✅ Verified facts

  • Who sells it: BioDynamix, fronted by Dr. Mark Weis. The BBB lists "Joint genesis" at 7362 Remcon Cir, El Paso, TX 79912 (BBB profile). An independent audit notes the label also shows a distributor address in Aurora, CO (DoseAudit).
  • BBB rating: A, but not BBB accredited. The BBB file was opened 2/23/2024 (BBB profile, checked October 8, 2026).
  • Refund: a 180-day "empty bottle" money-back guarantee on every package, per the vendor's official site (jointgenesis.com). Orders are processed by ClickBank, and refund requests go to the seller's support (details on the packaging) or through clkbank.com.
  • Real price: the sales copy mentions a $59 single bottle, but an independent checkout audit found the cheapest real package was 2 bottles for $158 (3 for $207, 6 for $294) (DoseAudit, checked September 28, 2026).
  • Chicken / egg allergy caution: Mobilee is derived from rooster (chicken) comb. If you have a chicken or poultry allergy, or an egg allergy, ask your doctor before taking it.
  • Blood thinners: ginger and boswellia may interact with anticoagulants such as warfarin. If you take a blood thinner or have a bleeding disorder or surgery coming up, talk to your doctor or pharmacist first.
  • Evidence: the Mobilee and pine bark doses match the amounts used in published trials, but several Mobilee studies involve researchers tied to its manufacturer (DoseAudit).
  • Regulators: we found no FDA warning letter, recall or FTC action naming Joint Genesis (searches run October 2026). That is not the same as FDA approval: dietary supplements are not evaluated by the FDA for safety or effectiveness.
💊 Not medical advice. Joint Genesis is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for arthritis or any disease. Diagnosed joint conditions need a doctor's care. Check with your doctor before starting any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, take prescription medication or have allergies.

Who might consider it, and who should skip it

✔ Might suit you if…

You want a joint supplement with every dose disclosed and a long refund window, and your doctor is happy with the ingredients.

✘ Skip it if…

You have a chicken, poultry or egg allergy, take blood thinners without medical advice, or were sold on the $59 single-bottle price.
