What to check before you buy in 🌿 Whole-Body Health

We have not tested Nitric Boost Ultra, so we will not tell you what it does. What we can do is tell you what goes wrong in this category, which is where the money usually goes:

  • Supplements are not evaluated by the FDA before sale, so the label tells you what is in the bottle and nothing about whether it does anything.
  • Check for interactions with anything you are already prescribed — the gap between "natural" and "inert" is much smaller than marketing suggests.
