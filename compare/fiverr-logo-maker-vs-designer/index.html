The honest case for the AI maker

If you are pre-revenue, testing a project name, or need something acceptable on a business card tomorrow, the $30 package is rational. It is not a brand identity, but it is faster than any human and cheaper than most gigs. The $60/$90 tiers add file formats and variations you will actually need (transparent PNG, vector on top tiers).

The catch: exclusivity

AI logo makers generate from template components. Your mark is yours to use, but similar marks can exist elsewhere — and if your business grows, a lookalike logo is a real problem for trademark and distinction. A human designer's custom mark (with source files and a paper trail of originality) is what trademark lawyers prefer to see. If you plan to register a trademark or build a recognizable brand, pay the human from day one.

The middle path most buyers miss

Use the $30 AI logo to validate the business, then hire a designer for the real identity once revenue exists. Our verified logo designers guide shows the $25–$130 band where 4.8★+ sellers with hundreds of reviews live — the professional tier without agency pricing. Bring your AI logo as a style reference and the brief writes itself.

First time buying on Fiverr?

Buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on orders under $200 — and a $200 order costs less than a $199 one (exact math here). Your money sits in escrow until you approve the delivery, and for anything over $500 you should split the work into milestones. The single best habit: order the seller's smallest package as a paid test before committing your real budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fiverr Logo Maker worth it?

For pre-revenue projects and quick tests, yes — $30 buys a usable logo in minutes. For a long-term brand, no: template-based marks are not exclusive and are weaker for trademark purposes. Serious brands should hire a designer.

Can I trademark an AI-generated logo?

You can file, but template-based marks carry higher risk of similarity conflicts. Custom work from a human designer with source files gives you a cleaner originality trail. Consult a trademark attorney before filing either way.

Why do designers charge $25 when the AI charges $30?

Because they sell different things: the AI sells instant template output; the designer sells a custom concept, revisions, and exclusive rights. The $25–$130 designer band is the professional sweet spot on Fiverr.
