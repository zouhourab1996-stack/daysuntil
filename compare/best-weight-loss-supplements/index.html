What we could not verify

Two limits apply to every comparison here. The first is time: we cannot test how any of these hold up over months of real use, so scores describe the product as it is now rather than as it will be. The second is access: where a capability sits behind a sales call or an enterprise plan, we could not test it directly and we say so rather than repeating the vendor's description of it. Where a specific claim could not be checked against two independent sources, it is flagged in the review instead of being quietly repeated.

Frequently asked questions

Do any of these weight-loss brands share third-party test results?

As of our October 2026 updates, none of the eight brands above publishes batch third-party test results on its sales page. If a Certificate of Analysis matters to you, request one from support before ordering — hesitation or silence is your answer.

Are these supplements FDA approved?

No — and no honest seller claims otherwise. Dietary supplements are regulated post-market, not pre-approved like drugs. Any weight-loss supplement claiming FDA approval is a red flag in itself.

Will any of them work without diet changes?

None of these products replaces calorie balance, protein intake or activity. Our reviews score honesty of marketing as heavily as anything else — sales pages promising weight loss without lifestyle change get scored down for it.
