Why people are actually leaving Upwork

Three recurring reasons in 2026: fee stacking (marketplace fee up to 7.99% + per-contract initiation + payment processing), Connects inflation for freelancers ($0.15 each, priced into rates), and budget compression as companies cut rates in AI-disrupted categories. Clients feel it as quotes that quietly include the freelancer's overhead.

The fee math on a $100 job

Upwork Basic with card payment: ~$7.99 marketplace fee + a $0.99–$14.99 initiation fee — call it $9–$23 before processing. Fiverr: $9.00 flat (5.5% + $3.50), no initiation fee, escrow included. On repeat weekly hires, Upwork's per-contract fees compound; on large single contracts, its 3% ACH rate can win. Run your own numbers with our fee calculator.

When Fiverr is the wrong alternative

Honesty cuts both ways: if you need a long-term embedded contractor with weekly hours and evolving scope, Upwork's contract model fits better than Fiverr's gig packages. Fiverr wins when the job is definable — a build, a design, an audit — because packages force scope clarity that hourly contracts often lack.

First time buying on Fiverr?

Buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on orders under $200 — and a $200 order costs less than a $199 one (exact math here). Your money sits in escrow until you approve the delivery, and for anything over $500 you should split the work into milestones. The single best habit: order the seller's smallest package as a paid test before committing your real budget.

Frequently asked questions

What does Upwork charge clients in 2026?

A marketplace fee of up to 7.99% on card/PayPal payments (3% for eligible US bank transfers, 10% on Business Plus), plus a contract initiation fee of $0.99–$14.99 per new contract. Payment processing fees can add more.

Is Fiverr cheaper than Upwork for clients?

On typical small jobs, yes: Fiverr charges 5.5% plus $3.50 under $200 with no initiation fee, while Upwork adds up to 7.99% plus a per-contract fee. On large contracts paid via US bank transfer, Upwork's 3% can be cheaper.

Which Upwork alternative is best for small businesses?

Fiverr for defined projects (fixed price, escrow, no proposals to review), PeoplePerHour or Guru for hourly-style European work, and Toptal when budget allows $60–$250/hr for senior specialists.
