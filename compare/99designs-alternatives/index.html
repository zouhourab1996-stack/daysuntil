Route 1: Fiverr gigs — the default answer

Verified Fiverr logo gigs run $10–$385: $10–$40 buys a clean PNG mark, $25–$130 is the professional band where 4.8★+ sellers with hundreds of reviews live, and $150–$385 buys brand-identity work with full file packs. You give up the contest's 30-concept exploration but gain speed (1–5 days) and iteration with a single accountable designer. Start with our 12 verified designers — real prices, real review counts.

Routes 2–3: when a template is honestly fine

The Logo Maker vs designer comparison covers this in depth: $30–$90 (Fiverr) or $20–$65 (standalone AI tools) buys instant, template-based marks. Fine for pre-revenue tests; wrong for trademark-bound brands. This is the honest tier where "cheaper" means "temporary."

Routes 4–5: the upgrade paths

Independent freelancers ($500–$2,500) cost more than a Bronze contest but sell strategy, brand guides and a relationship — the right buy when the name has to survive a decade. Unlimited subscriptions ($700–$1,400/mo) beat every option on cost-per-design once you need three or more designs monthly (per DDIY's 2026 pricing analysis).

First time buying on Fiverr?

Buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on orders under $200 — and a $200 order costs less than a $199 one (exact math here). Your money sits in escrow until you approve the delivery, and for anything over $500 you should split the work into milestones. The single best habit: order the seller's smallest package as a paid test before committing your real budget.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest good alternative to 99designs?

A $25–$130 Fiverr logo gig from a Top Rated seller — verified gigs in that band deliver custom work, source files and revisions within 1–5 days. Below $25 quality becomes a lottery.

Does 99designs charge fees on top of package prices?

Yes — a 5% client platform fee is added at checkout. On a $299 Bronze contest that is about $14.95 extra, and it is not shown prominently on the pricing page.

Are design subscriptions better than 99designs?

Only at volume. Unlimited subscriptions run $700–$1,400/month, so they beat contest pricing once you need roughly three or more designs per month. For one logo, they are dramatically more expensive.
