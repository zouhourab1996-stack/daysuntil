Privacy Policy

TrustScore is a static website. We do not run accounts, we do not ask for personal information, and we do not store anything you read or click.

Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to count anonymous visits. If advertising is enabled, Google AdSense may serve cookies for ad measurement. You can control personalized advertising in your Google settings. Affiliate links set tracking cookies (typically 60 days) with the networks we link to — that is how purchases are attributed to this site.

Third-party links

Clicking out to a vendor means their privacy policy applies from that moment on. We only link to products we have reviewed.

Contact

Contact us for any privacy question.

Third parties in more detail

We use Google AdSense for advertising. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on a profile of your interests across the sites you visit, which is how interest-based advertising works across the web generally. We may also load an analytics script that records that a page was viewed and roughly where from.

Neither of these sees anything you type into the tools on this site. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not transmitted to us or to anyone else. What advertising partners can observe is that you visited a page — not what you entered on it.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's ad settings, or industry-wide through the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance. Blocking third-party cookies in your browser achieves the same for most purposes, and the site works either way.

Your rights

We hold very little personal data, which makes most requests short. To be explicit:

  • Access and erasure. If you are in the UK, the EU or a similar jurisdiction you can ask what we hold and have it deleted. Because the tools store nothing and we do not run accounts, there is usually nothing to produce — and we will tell you that rather than staying silent.
  • Opting out of sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on our side.
  • Complaints. Email us first; if our answer does not satisfy you, your local data protection authority is the escalation route.
