Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

daysuntil.bond is reader-supported. The site may display advertising (including Google AdSense) and may contain affiliate links — links to products or services from which we may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no additional cost to you.

Astronomical data and countdown dates are never influenced by advertisers. Sponsored or affiliate content, where it appears, will be identifiable.

How the commission actually works

When you follow one of our links and then buy something, the seller's network records that the visit came from us and pays us a share. That share comes out of the seller's margin, not out of your price — you pay the same using our link as going directly, and occasionally less, because some links carry a discount the public listing does not.

If you would rather we earned nothing, type the seller's address in directly. The price is identical and we lose the commission, which is a perfectly reasonable preference.

What commission cannot buy here

Three things, stated plainly because they are the ones that matter. It cannot move a date: every countdown on this site is computed from the event's own data, and an advertiser has no mechanism and no route to change it. It cannot add or remove an event: the calendar is built from what we can verify. And it cannot change which countdown appears where — ordering follows the date.

Advertising pays for the site to exist. It does not get a vote on what it says.

Who pays, and where

Advertising on the site is served by Google AdSense, which fills the ad slots and pays us for the impressions. Separately, some pages carry affiliate links — mostly to products reviewed by sites in our network. Both are the same arrangement any ad-supported publisher has, and neither is hidden: the ad slots are labelled, and affiliate links on review pages carry a disclosure above them rather than in the footer where nobody reads it.

Editorial independence, stated plainly

No advertiser has reviewed or approved anything on this site, and none has been shown it in advance. Dates come from the event data, text is written by us, and the two do not meet. If a page ever carries sponsored placement, it will say so on the page itself rather than relying on you to infer it.
