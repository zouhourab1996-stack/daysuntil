How we judge products in this category

Supplements are not evaluated by the FDA before they are sold, which changes how a category like this has to be judged. Evidence has to come from the ingredient literature rather than the label, so we read the panel first and discount anything hidden inside a proprietary blend. We discount testimonials entirely — they are not data. What moves a score here: ingredient transparency with stated doses, a refund route that does not depend on the vendor replying, no disease claims on the sales page, and pricing that is stated rather than revealed at checkout.

FAQ

Do any of these supplements actually work? +
Some ingredients have published evidence behind them and most do not. Each review links what we found. No supplement on this site is FDA-evaluated for effectiveness, so treat the label as a list of ingredients and nothing more.
Are they safe? +
Supplements are not evaluated for safety before sale either, and several products in this category have been found to contain undeclared drug ingredients. If you take prescription medication, check for interactions with a pharmacist first.
How do refunds work? +
Most of these sell through ClickBank, which carries a 60-day guarantee you can claim through clkbank.com without the vendor. Each review states the route.
