How we judge products in this category

Psychic and reading offers are scored on the things that actually go wrong: unclear per-minute billing, auto-renewing credit packs, fear-based upsells and refunds that depend on a vendor answering email. We treat the reading itself as entertainment and do not score its accuracy, because there is nothing to measure. What costs a product its score in this category is nearly always billing behaviour, not the content of the reading.

FAQ

Are psychic readings real? +
There is no evidence base for them and we do not score them on accuracy. What we can assess — and do — is whether the billing is honest and the refund works.
What is the most common problem? +
Per-minute billing that escalates, and auto-renewal on credit packs. Both are noted in each review where they apply.
Should I make decisions based on a reading? +
No. Treat it as entertainment. If a reading is steering a medical, financial or relationship decision, that is the point to stop.
