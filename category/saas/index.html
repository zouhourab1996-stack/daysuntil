How we judge products in this category

Software is judged on the things that turn out to matter after month three, which are rarely the items on the feature list. Pricing transparency comes first: whether you can see the real cost at the seat count you will actually have, rather than a headline price that assumes one user. Data export is second — if you cannot get your own data out in a usable format on a plan you can afford, the tool is a hostage rather than a subscription. We also check whether there is a self-serve trial or only a sales demo, whether security documentation exists for anyone handling customer data, and whether the vendor's marketing describes what the product does today rather than a roadmap.

FAQ

How do you score SaaS tools? +
On pricing transparency, whether you can export your own data, whether a self-serve trial exists, and whether the security documentation is real. Features matter far less than these four, because they are what you discover after you have already committed.
What is the biggest trap? +
Seat-based pricing that looks cheap at one user and expensive at ten, combined with no usable export. Check both before signing an annual plan.
Do you test these yourselves? +
Where a self-serve trial exists, yes. Where access is behind a sales call, we say so in the review rather than repeating the vendor description.
