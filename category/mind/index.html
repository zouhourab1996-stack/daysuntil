FAQ

Are numerology reports accurate? +
They are not making a claim that can be accurate. Personalised readings feel precise for a documented reason — the Forer effect — and we say so in each review rather than implying the personalisation is evidence of anything.
Is any of this harmful? +
It stops being harmless when a reading is used to make medical, financial or relationship decisions. We flag any product that crosses that line.
How are they scored? +
On price transparency, whether the personalisation is real, whether the refund works, and whether the product stays inside entertainment rather than making claims it cannot support.
