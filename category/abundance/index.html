How we judge products in this category

There is no clinical evidence for manifestation programmes, and we do not pretend otherwise when scoring them. That means the score is not measuring whether the method works, because nothing could. It is measuring whether the offer is honest about what it is: clear pricing with no surprise upsell chain, a refund window honoured in practice, and marketing that does not promise specific financial outcomes. A programme that is upfront about being structured self-reflection scores better here than one that claims to change your bank balance.

FAQ

Do manifestation programmes work? +
Not in any measurable sense, and we do not score them as though they might. What we score is whether the offer is honest about being structured self-reflection rather than a mechanism.
Why do some still score well? +
Because a programme can be cheap, clearly priced, easy to refund and genuinely useful as a daily reflective practice. That is a real product even though the mechanism it claims is not.
What should I watch for? +
Upsell chains after a low entry price, outcome claims about money, and refund windows that are shorter than they appear in the sales copy.
