How Fiverr's buyer fee works

Fiverr's help center says: "The standard service fee is 5.5% of your purchase amount, and we add a $3.50 fee to orders under $200. Every payment you make carries its own service fee, so if your order involves multiple transactions, such as an extra or a tip, each one has a separate fee." (Fiverr Help Center: Paying for orders, extras, or custom offers, checked October 8, 2026.) Fiverr's payment terms date this structure to September 2025. Three ways to pay less in fees

  • Add extras at checkout, not later. Extras bought on an open order are a new payment with a new fee.
  • Bundle small jobs. Two $50 orders cost $12.50 in fees; one $100 custom offer costs $9.00.
  • Watch the $196.70 to $199.99 zone. Orders in that range cost more in total than a $200 order. Ask the seller for a $200 custom offer with one more revision instead.

Frequently asked questions
How much is Fiverr's buyer fee in 2026?

Fiverr adds a service fee of 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus a $3.50 fee on orders under $200. Orders of $200 or more pay only the 5.5%. Fiverr's help center states this fee, and you can review it at checkout before you pay.

Do tips and extras get charged a fee too?

Yes. Fiverr says every payment carries its own service fee, so an extra added later or a tip is charged a separate fee. Adding extras at checkout, as part of the original order, means only one fee.

Why does a $199 order cost more than a $200 order?

Because the $3.50 small-order fee stops at $200. A $199 order costs $213.45 in total; a $200 order costs $211.00. Any order from about $196.70 to $199.99 costs more than a $200 order.

How much will I pay for a $50 gig on Fiverr?

$56.25: the $50 gig, plus a 5.5% fee ($2.75), plus the $3.50 small-order fee.

Is this calculator exact?

It applies Fiverr's published fee rule and rounds to the cent. Taxes such as VAT, currency conversion and promotional credits can change your final total, so the checkout screen is always the final word.

How much does Fiverr take from sellers?

A flat 20% commission on everything — the order, extras and tips. Withdrawals add $0–$3 depending on the payout method. A $100 order leaves the seller $80 before withdrawal costs.

Do the buyer fees apply to extras and tips too?

Yes. The 5.5% service fee (and the $3.50 small-order fee under $200) applies to the total you pay — gig price plus extras plus tip. Budget the fee on the full cart, not the headline price.

Are Fiverr's buyer fees lower than Upwork's?

On typical jobs, yes: Fiverr charges 5.5% plus $3.50 under $200 with no initiation fee, while Upwork buyers pay up to 7.99% in marketplace fees plus $0.99–$14.99 per new contract. The full comparison — including when Upwork is actually cheaper — is in our Upwork alternatives guide.
