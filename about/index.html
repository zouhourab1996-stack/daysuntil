About TrustScore

TrustScore is an independent review site for digital products in the spiritual-personal-growth space — soulmate readings, numerology, astrology, archetypes, manifestation, health supplements and B2B software. The space is loud, anonymous and full of five-star copy-paste reviews; we exist to be the opposite: named methodology, published cons, dated updates, one comparable score.

The site is part of a small independent network that includes twinflame.bond, prophetic.pw, daysuntil.bond and bmrcalc.bond.

What we are trying to be

Most review sites in this space are affiliate funnels wearing the costume of a review: a score that always lands between 8 and 10, a "cons" section with one cosmetic complaint in it, and a link. That model works, which is why it is everywhere. It also makes the scores useless, because a scale that never goes below 8 is not measuring anything.

So the two things we hold to are these. First, the scores really do spread — a product we think is poor gets a poor score and we say why, even when it is a product we link to. Second, where a claim cannot be checked against two independent sources, we say so instead of repeating it. That second rule is the one that costs us the most pages, and it is the reason the output is slower than it looks like it should be.

What we are not

We are not a testing laboratory, and we do not have the budget of one. Where we have tested a product ourselves we label it; where a review is built from research, vendor documentation and user reports, we label that too. Both are honest, and pretending the second is the first is where review sites lose their credibility.

We are also not neutral about the products' marketing. If a sales page makes a claim the evidence does not support, we will say so in plain language rather than laundering it into "some users report".
