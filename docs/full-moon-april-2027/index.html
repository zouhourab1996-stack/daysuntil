About April 2027 Full Moon

The April 2027 full moon peaks at 22:28 UTC on April 20, 2027, at 0°37' Scorpio in the tropical zodiac. Full moons always rise at sunset and set at sunrise, so the best viewing is any time the Moon is up that night — no equipment needed.

In astrology, the full moon in Scorpio is considered the month's closing chapter: what began at the Scorpio new moon six months earlier reaches fullness, and what is no longer working becomes impossible to ignore. Monthly full moons are the sky's built-in review meetings.
