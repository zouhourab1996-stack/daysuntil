How these dates are worked out

Sporting fixtures are scheduled years ahead and are the most reliable dates on this site — a Super Bowl or an Olympic opening ceremony is fixed by contract and broadcast deal long in advance. The one thing that does move is kickoff time, which is set for a television audience and can shift by an hour or three depending on the territory. The date, unlike the start time, essentially never changes.

Why these are the most reliable dates here

A major final or an opening ceremony is fixed by contract and broadcast deal years ahead — a host city is awarded, a venue is booked, and television rights are sold against specific dates. That makes sporting fixtures considerably firmer than anything else on this site, including astronomical events, which shift by minutes as the calculations are refined.

The one thing that does move is the start time, and it moves for television rather than for sport: broadcasters shift kickoff to suit their audience, sometimes by several hours. So the date you are counting down to is stable, and the hour you need to be free is not. If the fixture matters, confirm the local start time closer to the day.

FAQ

Are these dates confirmed?

Yes. Major finals and opening ceremonies are contracted years ahead of time.

Do kickoff times change?

They can, and broadcasters adjust them for their audience. The date is stable; the hour is not.

Why is the countdown in my time zone?

So it matches your own clock. A fixture that starts Sunday evening in the United States starts early Monday morning in Europe.

What about events that get postponed?

Rare, but it happens. If a fixture moves we update the countdown to the rescheduled date.

How far ahead are sporting fixtures set?

Major finals and opening ceremonies are contracted years in advance, which makes them among the most reliable dates on this site. Kickoff times are set much later, for television.

What happens if a fixture is postponed?

It is rare for a final. Where it happens we update the countdown to the rescheduled date rather than leaving the old one running.
