What happens at the Olympic Closing Ceremony?

The Closing Ceremony marks the end of the Olympic Games. Athletes gather for the final celebration, the Olympic flag is handed to the next host city, and the Olympic Flame is extinguished. It is the formal closing moment after the final competition sessions.

The LA28 ceremony is planned for the LA Memorial Coliseum, a venue with a long Olympic history. The official schedule places the final day of competition and the Closing Ceremony on July 30.

Do not confuse the Olympic and Paralympic closing ceremonies

LA28 hosts both the Olympic and Paralympic Games, and their closing dates are different. The Olympic Closing Ceremony is scheduled for July 30, 2028. The Paralympic Games run from August 15 to August 27, with their own Closing Ceremony on August 27.

The official LA28 Games dates announcement lists the two event periods separately. This countdown is specifically for the Olympic Closing Ceremony, not the Paralympic finale.

What time is 6 p.m. Pacific in other time zones?

The published start time is 6:00 p.m. Pacific Daylight Time in Los Angeles on July 30, 2028. That is 9:00 p.m. Eastern on July 30 and 01:00 UTC on July 31. In many European and African time zones, the local calendar date will therefore be July 31.

The countdown uses the official Pacific start instant and converts it for your device's time zone. This avoids treating the ceremony as if it began at midnight in every country.
