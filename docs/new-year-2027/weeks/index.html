Why the week count moves the way it does

Weeks are the unit people actually plan in. Deadlines, notice periods and rotas run in weeks, which is why a week count tends to be more useful than a raw day count once you are more than a fortnight out. Note that the two figures answer slightly different questions: "how many weeks" is full weeks only, while "weeks and days" keeps the remainder.

FAQ

How many weeks until New Year 2027?

10 weeks, as of today. New Year 2027 falls on Friday, January 1, 2027.

Is that full weeks or rounded?

Full weeks: 10. The remaining 0 days do not add up to a whole one, which is why the plain week count and the weeks-and-days count differ.

When does the week count tick over?

Every seven days, counted to the second. The countdown runs in your own time zone rather than UTC, so it changes when your clock says it should.

What date is New Year 2027?

Friday, January 1, 2027 — a Friday.
