About June 2028 New Moon

The June 2028 new moon falls at 18:29 UTC on June 22, 2028, at 1°51' Cancer. The Moon sits between Earth and Sun, so its dark side faces us: no Moon visible for about a day on either side.

A new moon in Cancer is the traditional planting moment of the month — the sky's blank page. Anything begun in the days around it shares the sign's flavor, and the intentions you set now come to full light at the Cancer full moon six months later.
