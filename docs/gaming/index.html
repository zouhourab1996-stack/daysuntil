How these dates are worked out

Game release dates are announcements, not astronomical events, which makes them a different kind of countdown. A date is real when the publisher has put it in a financial filing or a dated trailer, and provisional until then. Slips are normal and usually land two to six months after the original window, so treat any date more than a year out as a placeholder. Where a title has a confirmed date and a previous slipped one, we count to the confirmed date.

How we decide a release date is real

A date earns a place on this site when it comes from the publisher directly — a dated trailer, a financial filing, or a store listing the publisher controls. A date found only on a retailer page or in a rumour round-up does not, because those are wrong often enough to be worse than useless. Where a title has both a confirmed date and an earlier one it has since missed, we count to the confirmed one and say which is which.

Unlock times are the other half of the question and work differently. Most launches unlock at a stated global moment, which lands at a different local hour depending on where you are counting from, so two people can be counting to the same instant and see it fall on different days.

FAQ

Are these release dates confirmed?

Only where stated. A date quoted from a publisher announcement is firmer than one quoted from a retailer listing, and we label which is which.

Do release dates slip often?

Frequently. Industry-wide, a majority of titles with dates more than a year out move at least once.

Why is the countdown in my time zone?

Because unlocks are usually simultaneous worldwide at a stated UTC hour, so your local unlock time depends on where you are.

What if the date changes after you publish?

We update the page. If you spot a slip before we do, the about page has a contact route.

Why do game release dates slip so often?

Because a date is a marketing commitment made before the work is finished. A slip is usually a sign the studio wants the game to work rather than a sign of trouble, and it is common enough that we treat any date more than a year out as provisional.

Do release times differ by platform?

Often. Console and PC unlocks are frequently staggered, and a simultaneous global unlock is the exception. Check the platform-specific time before planning around it.
