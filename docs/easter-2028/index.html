About Easter 2028

Easter 2028 falls on Sunday, April 16, 2028. The most important feast in Christianity, celebrating the resurrection — eggs, bunnies and spring festivals came along later. Easter is set by the first Sunday after the first full moon of spring, which is why it moves every year.
