Why the week count moves the way it does

Weeks are the unit people actually plan in. Deadlines, notice periods and rotas run in weeks, which is why a week count tends to be more useful than a raw day count once you are more than a fortnight out. Note that the two figures answer slightly different questions: "how many weeks" is full weeks only, while "weeks and days" keeps the remainder.

About Uranus Retrograde September 2027

Uranus stations retrograde on September 15, 2027 at 07:21 UTC, at 9°57' Gemini — computed to the minute with our own Keplerian ephemeris.

Uranus retrograde turns the planet of disruption and freedom inward for about five months each year — rebellion becomes review. The station is the turning point itself: the day the planet's apparent motion reaches zero before reversing. In tradition, days around a station carry the event's themes most intensely — decisions made at a station are said to set the tone for the whole season that follows.
