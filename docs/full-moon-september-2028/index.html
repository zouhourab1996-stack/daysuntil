About September 2028 Full Moon

The September 2028 full moon peaks at 23:49 UTC on September 03, 2028, at 11°59' Pisces in the tropical zodiac. Full moons always rise at sunset and set at sunrise, so the best viewing is any time the Moon is up that night — no equipment needed.

In astrology, the full moon in Pisces is considered the month's closing chapter: what began at the Pisces new moon six months earlier reaches fullness, and what is no longer working becomes impossible to ignore. Monthly full moons are the sky's built-in review meetings.
