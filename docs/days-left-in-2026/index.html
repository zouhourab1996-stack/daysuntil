How Many Days Are Left in 2026?

As of Tuesday, November 3, 2026, there are 58 days left in 2026, which is 8 weeks and 2 days. Take out weekends and the US federal holidays still ahead and you are left with 39 working days. The figures recalculate from your own clock when the page loads.

"Days left" here means the days after today, so the number reads 0 on December 31. Count today as a full day and every figure in the first rows goes up by one.

Most online day counters stop at the weekday row and add a footnote saying holidays are not subtracted. The working-day row is the one this page adds, because it is the number people planning a deadline actually need.

Do it in a spreadsheet

Days left, today excluded: =DATE(2026,12,31)-TODAY()

Working days left after the three holidays, with their dates typed into cells H2:H4: =NETWORKDAYS(TODAY()+1,DATE(2026,12,31),H2:H4)

Skip the holiday range and the second formula returns the plain weekday count that most online counters show.

Why two counters show different numbers

Today in or out. Some tools count today as day one, so their answer is one higher than ours on the same morning.

December 31 in or out. The last day of the year is either the final day to count or the finish line. Here it is the finish line, so the figure is 0 on December 31 itself.

Time zones. A page that counts on a server clock can be a day ahead or behind for you. This one reads your device's date.

Old pages. A static number printed without a date goes stale the next morning. If a counter does not say what day it was calculated for, check it against the table above.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are left in 2026?

As of Tuesday, November 3, 2026, there are 58 days left in 2026 (8 weeks and 2 days), counting the days after today up to and including December 31. Count today as well and the figure is 59. The number on this page recalculates from your device's date, so it stays right every day until the year ends.

How many working days are left in 2026 in the US?

As of Tuesday, November 3, 2026, there are 42 weekdays left, and 3 of them are US federal holidays (Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11; Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26; Christmas Day on Friday, December 25), which leaves 39 working days. Your employer may close on other days, such as the Friday after Thanksgiving, so treat the figure as a baseline.

How many full moons are left in 2026?

Two: Tuesday, November 24 and Thursday, December 24. The December one peaks at about 01:30 UTC on the 24th, which is still the evening of Wednesday, December 23 in North America.

Does the count include today and December 31?

Not here. "Days left" means the days after today through December 31, so the figure reads 0 on New Year's Eve. Counting today as a full day adds one. Many counters do the opposite, which is the most common reason two sites show different numbers on the same morning.
