How this countdown calculator works

Enter a name and target date. If you provide a time, the calculator counts to that exact local date and time. If you leave the time blank, it uses midnight at the beginning of the selected date in your device's time zone. The live display updates once per second.

What the numbers mean

  • Days, hours, minutes and seconds: the remaining duration, broken into familiar units.
  • Total hours: the complete duration expressed as hours, including partial days.
  • Weeks: the remaining duration divided into seven-day periods.
  • Weekdays and weekend days: calendar dates crossed between now and the target. These are not business days and do not exclude public holidays.

Time zones and accuracy

The calculator uses the clock and time-zone rules provided by your browser. The calculation runs locally and no form submission or account is involved. When you choose to share, the event name, date and optional time are placed after the # in the link. Browsers do not send that fragment in the page request; the receiving browser reads it locally and recreates the countdown. For an event tied to a specific global instant, agree on the intended time zone before sharing.

Privacy by design

Your custom event is not stored in an account or sent to a countdown API. A share link includes the name and target date/time you entered, and anyone with the link can read those details. Do not include private or sensitive information in an event name. Hosting and analytics providers may still process ordinary visit information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Common questions

Can I count down to a past date?

Yes. The display changes to an elapsed-time counter and shows how long has passed since the selected date.

Why can two people see different hours?

If you choose a local date and time, each browser interprets that time in its own time zone. To coordinate across locations, include the time zone in the event name or agree on a shared reference time.

Does it count public holidays as weekdays?

Yes. The weekday figure means Monday through Friday only. It does not know which public holidays apply in your country or workplace.

Is my countdown stored?

No. The calculator runs in your browser. A share link can recreate the countdown, but the site does not keep a copy of your custom event.
