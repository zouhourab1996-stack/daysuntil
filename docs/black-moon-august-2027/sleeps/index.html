How a sleep count works

The convention: one sleep means one night still to go. On the morning before, the answer is one. On the morning of the day itself, it is zero — you have run out of nights to sleep through. Because it tracks nights rather than 24-hour periods, the figure can read one higher than the day count for an event that lands in the morning.

About Black Moon August 2027

August 31, 2027 at 17:42 UTC brings a second new moon in a single calendar month — a Black Moon, the new moon's answer to the Blue. It happens only every two to three years, which is exactly why the phrase 'once in a blue moon' means what it means.

This one peaks at 8°06' Virgo. Nothing looks different from an ordinary full or new moon — the rarity is purely calendrical, and the counter above treats it like any other: with perfect accuracy.

Is the number of sleeps the same as the number of days?
