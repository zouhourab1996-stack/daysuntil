About Jupiter Direct May 2028

Jupiter stations direct on May 13, 2028 at 20:09 UTC, at 17°34' Virgo — computed to the minute with our own Keplerian ephemeris.

Jupiter retrograde is the four-month review of growth, beliefs and plans — about a third of every year, every year. The station is the turning point itself: the day the planet's apparent motion reaches zero before reversing. In tradition, days around a station carry the event's themes most intensely — decisions made at a station are said to set the tone for the whole season that follows.
