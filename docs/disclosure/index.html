Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

daysuntil.bond is free to use and has Google AdSense advertising code integrated on eligible content pages. Ads may be selected and placed automatically by Google. The site does not currently publish affiliate links or paid sponsored articles.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies as described in our Privacy Policy. The public ads.txt file identifies sellers authorised for this domain; a seller being listed does not mean that it serves an ad on every visit.

Astronomical data, event dates and editorial decisions are not sold to advertisers. Sponsored content, if introduced in future, will be clearly labelled.

What advertising cannot buy here

Advertising cannot move a date: countdowns are calculated from the event data, not from an advertiser's preference. It cannot buy a favourable event description or a place in the calendar. Editorial decisions and advertising are kept separate.

Who pays, and where

Google AdSense is the advertising integration currently deployed on the site. Other authorised sellers or resellers may appear in the supply chain where listed in ads.txt; the file is a transparency record, not a promise that each listed seller serves every impression.

Editorial independence, stated plainly

No advertiser has reviewed or approved anything on this site, and none has been shown it in advance. Dates come from the event data, text is written by us, and the two do not meet. If a page ever carries sponsored placement, it will say so on the page itself rather than relying on you to infer it.
