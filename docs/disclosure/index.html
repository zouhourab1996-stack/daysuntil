Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

daysuntil.bond is free to use and supported by advertising served by Google and our authorised advertising partner Advergic. The site does not carry affiliate links or sponsored posts.

Astronomical data and countdown dates are never influenced by advertisers. Sponsored content, if it ever appears, will be clearly labelled.

What advertising cannot buy here

Three things, stated plainly because they are the ones that matter. Advertising cannot move a date: every countdown on this site is computed from the event's own data, and an advertiser has no mechanism and no route to change it. It cannot add or remove an event: the calendar is built from what we can verify. And it cannot change which countdown appears where — ordering follows the date.

Advertising pays for the site to exist. It does not get a vote on what it says.

Who pays, and where

Advertising on the site is served by Google and by Advergic, our authorised sales partner, which fill the labelled ad slots and pay us for the impressions. Every authorised seller is listed in our public ads.txt file.

Editorial independence, stated plainly

No advertiser has reviewed or approved anything on this site, and none has been shown it in advance. Dates come from the event data, text is written by us, and the two do not meet. If a page ever carries sponsored placement, it will say so on the page itself rather than relying on you to infer it.
