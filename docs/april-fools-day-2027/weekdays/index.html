What this count leaves out

This count is Monday to Friday only and does not strip out public holidays. Those vary by country, state and employer, so subtract the ones you actually get from the month that contains them. If you work a non-standard rota, the weekend column is the figure to adjust rather than the weekday column.

FAQ

How many weekdays until April Fools' Day 2027?

114 weekdays, with 46 days falling on a weekend, out of 160 days in total.

Does that include public holidays?

No. The count is Monday to Friday only. Public holidays differ by country and employer, so subtract them from the month-by-month table below to get your own figure.

Why count weekdays instead of days?

Because it is the number that maps onto real working time. A 60-day countdown sounds generous until you realise 16 of those days are a weekend.

What date is April Fools' Day 2027?

Thursday, April 1, 2027 — a Thursday.
