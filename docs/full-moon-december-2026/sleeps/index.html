How a sleep count works

The convention: one sleep means one night still to go. On the morning before, the answer is one. On the morning of the day itself, it is zero — you have run out of nights to sleep through. Because it tracks nights rather than 24-hour periods, the figure can read one higher than the day count for an event that lands in the morning.

About December 2026 Full Moon

The December 2026 full moon peaks at 01:30 UTC on December 24, 2026, at 2°14' Cancer in the tropical zodiac. Full moons always rise at sunset and set at sunrise, so the best viewing is any time the Moon is up that night — no equipment needed.

In astrology, the full moon in Cancer is considered the month's closing chapter: what began at the Cancer new moon six months earlier reaches fullness, and what is no longer working becomes impossible to ignore. Monthly full moons are the sky's built-in review meetings.
