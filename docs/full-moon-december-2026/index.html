About December 2026 Full Moon

The December 2026 full moon peaks at 01:30 UTC on December 24, 2026, at 2°14' Cancer in the tropical zodiac. Full moons always rise at sunset and set at sunrise, so the best viewing is any time the Moon is up that night — no equipment needed.
