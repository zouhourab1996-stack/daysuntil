How Many Days in 2027?

365 — a common year, not a leap year. Here is the full breakdown: every month, every weekday, how 2027 compares to 2026 and 2028, and why the year starting on a Friday matters for your planning.

The short answer

2027 has 365 days. It is a common year, not a leap year. That means:

  • Total days: 365
  • Weekdays (Mon–Fri): 261
  • Weekend days (Sat + Sun): 104 (52 Saturdays + 52 Sundays)
  • February 2027: 28 days (no February 29)
  • January 1, 2027: Friday
  • December 31, 2027: Friday

The reason is simple: 2027 is not divisible by 4. The leap year rule is that a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 — except for end-of-century years, which must also be divisible by 400. 2027 ÷ 4 = 506.75, so it fails the test. The next leap year is 2028, which will have 366 days.

Why 2027 starts on a Friday

2026 has 365 days and starts on a Thursday. Because 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day, the next year starts one day of the week later. So 2027 starts on a Friday. And because 2027 is also a common year (365 days), 2028 starts on a Saturday — and because 2028 is a leap year, it has 366 days and the pattern shifts again.

This is the standard Gregorian calendar progression. Every common year pushes the start day forward by one; every leap year pushes it forward by two. The recent sequence: 2024 started on a Monday and was a leap year (366 days → +2 days). 2025 started on a Wednesday. 2026 started on a Thursday. 2027 starts on a Friday and is a common year (365 days → +1 day). 2028 starts on a Saturday and is a leap year (366 days).

The day-of-week progression is deterministic. Each common year adds one day; each leap year adds two. The sequence is one of the few things in calendar math that you can compute by hand and always get right.

How to count the weekdays by hand

The 261 weekdays figure is not arbitrary. Here is how you get it:

365 days ÷ 7 = 52 weeks + 1 day. That gives you 52 of every weekday (52 × 5 = 260 weekdays) plus one extra weekday — the day of the week that January 1 falls on, which appears 53 times. In 2027, January 1 is a Friday, so there are 53 Fridays and 52 of every other weekday.

That makes the full weekday count: 52 Mondays + 52 Tuesdays + 52 Wednesdays + 52 Thursdays + 53 Fridays + 52 Saturdays + 52 Sundays = 365. Weekdays total: 261. Weekend days total: 104.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are there in 2027?

2027 has 365 days. It is a common year, not a leap year. 2027 is not divisible by 4, so it does not meet the leap year rule. That means February 2027 has 28 days, and the full year has the standard 365 days — 261 weekdays (Monday through Friday) and 104 weekend days (Saturdays and Sundays).

Is 2027 a leap year?

No, 2027 is not a leap year. The leap year rule is: a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except for end-of-century years which must also be divisible by 400. 2027 is not divisible by 4 (2027 ÷ 4 = 506.75), so it is a common year with 365 days. The next leap year after 2024 is 2028.

How many weekdays are in 2027?

2027 has 261 weekdays (Monday through Friday) and 104 weekend days (52 Saturdays + 52 Sundays). That is the standard split for any common year starting on a Friday, which 2027 does — January 1, 2027 is a Friday.

What day of the week is January 1, 2027?

January 1, 2027 is a Friday. Because 2027 starts on a Friday and is a common year (365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day), the year shifts by one day of the week from 2026. That means every date in 2027 falls one day later in the week than the same date in 2026.

How does 2027 compare to 2026 and 2028?

2026 has 365 days and starts on a Thursday. 2027 has 365 days and starts on a Friday — one day later. 2028 is a leap year with 366 days and starts on a Saturday. So the three-year sequence is: 365 (Thu start), 365 (Fri start), 366 (Sat start). The leap day in 2028 — February 29, 2028 — is the next February 29 anywhere until 2032.
