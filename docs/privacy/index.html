Privacy Policy

daysuntil.bond is a static website. We do not require accounts, we do not collect personal information, and the countdowns you view are not tied to you.

Cookies & analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics and advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) that use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can control personalized advertising through your Google settings. No countdown data or searches you type on this site are stored by us.

Third-party links

Our pages link to third-party sites (including sites in our network). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Contact

Questions? Reach us via any site in our network footer.

Advertising in more detail

We serve advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to build a profile of interests across the sites you visit, which is how interest-based advertising works generally on the web. None of it sees anything you do on a countdown page, because there is nothing to see: the countdowns are calculated on your device and no input is ever sent anywhere.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's ad settings, or industry-wide through the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance. Blocking third-party cookies in your browser achieves much the same, and every page here works without them.

Your rights

Because we hold no accounts and collect no personal information, there is normally nothing for us to produce, correct or delete — and we will tell you that plainly rather than going quiet. If you are in a jurisdiction with access or erasure rights and you want them exercised, reach us through any site in the network footer. If our answer does not satisfy you, your local data protection authority is the escalation route.

What the countdowns do with your device

Nothing beyond arithmetic. When a page loads, your browser works out the remaining time from the event date and the clock on your own machine. That is why the countdown stays accurate offline once the page has loaded, and why two people in different time zones see different numbers for the same event. No input is uploaded, no preference is stored beyond what your browser keeps for any web page, and closing the tab discards everything.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes materially, this page changes with it. We are not going to start collecting personal information quietly — the whole point of a static site is that it does not need to.

Children

The site is general-audience and not directed at children. Since nothing is collected in the first place, there is nothing to collect from a child either.
