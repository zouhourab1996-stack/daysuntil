About October 2027 New Moon

The October 2027 new moon falls at 13:38 UTC on October 29, 2027, at 5°54' Scorpio. The Moon sits between Earth and Sun, so its dark side faces us: no Moon visible for about a day on either side.

A new moon in Scorpio is the traditional planting moment of the month — the sky's blank page. Anything begun in the days around it shares the sign's flavor, and the intentions you set now come to full light at the Scorpio full moon six months later.
