Advertising & affiliate disclosure

TrustScore is reader-supported. When you buy through links on this site, we may earn an affiliate commission from the vendor at no additional cost to you. Most products here are sold through networks such as ClickBank and Reditus (B2B SaaS); commissions typically range from a few dollars upward per sale.

The rules that keep this honest:

1. Commissions never influence scores — including products we rate below 7.5 and still link to.

2. Every review page carries this disclosure at the point of the link, not just in a footer.

3. We do not accept payment for reviews, placement or score changes. Ever.

4. The site may also display contextual advertising (e.g., Google AdSense) if enabled; it will always be distinguishable from editorial content.

How the commission actually works

It is worth being specific, because "we may earn a commission" is vague in a way that hides the mechanics. When you click a link on this site and buy something, the seller's network records that the visit came from us and pays us a share. The two networks involved are ClickBank, which handles most of the consumer products, and Reditus, which handles the B2B SaaS listings.

The important part is where the money comes from. It is paid by the seller out of their margin, not added to your price. You do not pay more by using our link than by going directly — and in practice you usually pay slightly less, because some of our links carry a discount the public listing does not.

If you would rather we did not earn anything

Go to the vendor directly by typing their address into your browser. The price will be the same and we lose the commission, which is a completely reasonable thing to prefer. What we would ask is that you still read the review — the scoring is the same whether or not you use a link, and we publish negative findings about products we also link to.

Where the line is

Two things we do not do. We do not accept payment to write a review, and we do not let a commission rate affect where a product is placed in a comparison. Placement follows the score. Several products on this site are linked despite scoring below 7.5, because a mediocre product honestly described is more useful than a good one you cannot find.
