For brands & affiliate managers

We put products through the TrustScore methodology for free — and we publish the cons as loudly as the pros. We don't sell reviews, and payment can't remove criticism. To submit an offer for consideration, send the product, its live pricing page, refund terms and support SLA. If we take it on, we'll tell you exactly what we found — good and bad.

What happens after you write

Corrections come first. If a score, a price or a fee figure is wrong, we would rather fix it today than argue about it next month, and we will tell you what we found either way — including when you are right and we were not. Vendor enquiries and review requests get a considered reply, though a request for a better score is not one we can act on.

If you are reporting an error, include the page address and what you expected to see. That is usually enough for us to reproduce it.

What we can't do

  • Change a score because a vendor asks. Scores come from the published rubric on our methodology page. If you think a score is wrong, the route is evidence, not a request.
  • Remove an honest negative. If a review documents a real problem, it stays. Where we got something factually wrong we will correct it, and we will note the correction.
  • Advise on individual purchases. We can tell you what we found about a product. We cannot tell you whether it is right for your situation, because we do not know your situation.
  • Handle refunds on a vendor's behalf. We are not the seller. For ClickBank purchases the refund route is clkbank.com with your order number; for anything else it is the vendor.
